Samsung Electronics closed at KRW 232,000 on October 13, down 7.39%. SK hynix fell 10.09%, and the KOSPI broke below 6,000 intraday. Just five days earlier, Samsung had reported preliminary quarterly operating profit of KRW 107.4 trillion. Strong earnings and a sharp share-price decline arrived at the same time.

The most convincing explanation is that doubts about how long memory earnings can last coincided with a gap in domestic buying demand. CXMT's new-process announcement and concerns about funding for AI investment gave investors reasons to sell. The end of Samsung's share buyback reduced a source of demand that had absorbed some of that selling. Futures and program trading may have transmitted or amplified the shock.

This distinction matters when assessing a rebound. A temporary recovery is possible if the order imbalance eases. A sustained trend reversal would also require foreign cash-equity selling to slow and memory earnings expectations to stabilize.

## The selloff centered on memory; a rebound needs cash-equity flows

- Memory stocks also fell sharply in Japan and Taiwan. Samsung-specific bad news or a Korea-only exit from assets does not fully explain the move.
- Korean government bonds strengthened while USD/KRW rose KRW 10. The evidence is insufficient to call the equity selling a liquidation of all Korean assets. It also does not prove that country risk is absent.
- CXMT said it plans to unveil a server DDR5 product by year-end, but it did not disclose yield, shipment volume, or customer qualification. A technology announcement cannot be translated directly into a Samsung earnings decline.
- A technical rebound toward KRW 240,000–245,000 and a trend reversal accompanied by foreign investor inflows are different scenarios. A price bounce alone would not confirm a bottom.

The reference point is the October 13, 2026 close in Korea, Japan, and Taiwan. U.S. figures come from reports published during the October 13 session. This is a review prepared before the Korean market opened on October 14, not a report on the realized performance of a later forecast.

Over the five sessions from October 6 through October 13, individual investors bought KRW 4.1397 trillion of Samsung Electronics and KRW 3.3253 trillion of SK hynix. Samsung's 46.30% foreign ownership ratio is as of October 12. Presenting it as the October 13 closing ratio would move the date forward by one day. The lower ownership ratio allows two interpretations: less foreign-held stock remains available for sale, or foreign investors are structurally reducing their positions. Before cash-equity buying turns positive, the first interpretation alone is not enough to establish confidence in a rebound.

## The end of the buyback reduced demand independently of earnings

Samsung's share buyback ended on October 6. That date is distinct from the October 12 filing of the results report. Flow reports cited the end of the buyback as a factor that weakened a buffer against selling. The end of one buyback program does not mean that every future share repurchase has stopped.

A reproduced filing lists 56,856,305 shares acquired across 29 acquisition dates from August 24 through October 6, for approximately KRW 15 trillion. Dividing roughly KRW 15 trillion by 29 days gives an average of about KRW 517.2 billion per acquisition day. The detailed figures have not been finally reconciled against the original DART filing, so treat them as indicative. [Reproduction of the share acquisition results report](https://www.bosoop.com/disclosures/20261012000421/)

This average cannot be subtracted from one day's foreign net selling to estimate an expected price decline. The timing, price, and responsiveness of the actual orders differ. It is still reasonable to say that when a large, recurring source of demand diminishes, the same amount of selling can move prices more easily. Daishin Securities' analysis of the buyback buffer points to this channel as well. [Hankyung flow analysis](https://hankyung.com/article/2026101396276)

Samsung's official preliminary results were revenue of KRW 195 trillion and operating profit of KRW 107.4 trillion. Operating profit rose 20.01% from the previous quarter. A change in expectations about how long high earnings can last fits the share-price action better than the claim that reported earnings have already deteriorated. That does not mean a future earnings decline has been confirmed. [Samsung Newsroom, October 8](https://news.samsung.com/kr/삼성전자-2026년-3분기-잠정실적-발표)

There is a strong counterargument. A report citing Samsung Securities said September memory export prices fell 10.7% month over month, while export weight rose 27.2% and export value increased 13.5%. A decline in prices is difficult to equate with a collapse in end demand. These statistics may also reflect changes in product mix, so they cannot be applied directly to Samsung's selling prices or margins.

## Bond strength and won weakness limit the case for a broad exit

Judging country risk from equities alone can miss signals from other markets. Bonds and foreign exchange moved as follows on the same day.

| Indicator | October 13 |
|---|---:|
| Korean government bond, 3-year | 3.857%, -6.4 bp |
| Korean government bond, 10-year | 4.229%, -7.7 bp |
| 10-year minus 3-year government bond yield spread | 37.2 bp |
| Foreign net buying in 3-year / 10-year Treasury futures | +14,312 / +2,996 contracts |
| Consecutive sessions of foreign net buying in both futures | 6 sessions |
| USD/KRW daytime close | KRW 1,345.1, +KRW 10.0 |

Sources: [EToday bond-market close](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2635353), [MoneyToday foreign-exchange report](https://www.mt.co.kr/economy/2026/10/13/2026101315493144500). One basis point (bp) is 0.01 percentage point.

Government bond yields fell. Therefore, a same-day spike in domestic government bond yields is not a plausible explanation for the stock decline. Foreign buying of Treasury futures also weakens the claim that investors were reducing Korean assets across the board in the same direction.

However, there is no evidence that the sellers of equities and buyers of Treasury futures were the same investors. Contract counts cannot be netted against the value of equity selling. Safe-haven demand, expectations of slower growth, and separate bond-position adjustments may all have played a role. Strong government bonds alone do not prove that country risk is absent.

Dividing KRW 4.5259 trillion of foreign net equity selling by KRW 1,345.1 per dollar gives about USD 3.365 billion. The exchange-rate move was approximately 0.75%, calculated as KRW 10 divided by KRW 1,335.1. These are different kinds of figures. They do not mean all proceeds from stock sales were converted into dollars that day. Dollar supply from exporters, currency hedges, and settlement lags can change the exchange-rate response.

One interpretation is that won strength encouraged profit-taking; another is that exporters' dollar-denominated earnings matter more. The fact that foreign investors sold on October 12, when the won was strong, makes it difficult to explain the selling through exchange rates alone. If the won, government bonds, and credit markets all weaken together, the current interpretation of an equity-centered reduction in risk should be revisited.

## U.S. rates and AI financing are background pressures

Reports during the U.S. session on October 13 put the 10-year Treasury yield near 5.19%, Brent crude near USD 104.63, and WTI near USD 91.85. The USD 106.65 Brent figure in the Korean currency report was the U.S. close on October 12. Prices observed at different times should not be combined as if they were simultaneous. [Kitco, October 13 intraday report](https://kitco.com/news/article/2026-10-13/gold-firms-dollar-yields-and-oil-retreat-cpi-kitco-am-report)

High interest rates can increase the financing cost of AI infrastructure investment and reduce the present value of distant future earnings. But the absolute level of rates is different from a new rate shock on the day. These materials do not support attributing a specific share of Korea's selloff to U.S. rates.

Concerns about financing AI investment were mentioned in reports on Broadcom management's comments. The original statement was not rechecked, so this article does not quote it directly. The questions to track in company results are whether investment is funded with cash flow or more external borrowing, and whether customer revenue is keeping pace with higher investment.

## The futures selloff is visible, but the scale of forced selling is unknown

At 09:47:58 on October 13, the KOSPI 200 futures contract was down 5.08% from the previous close, triggering a sell-sidecar. This temporarily suspends the effectiveness of program sell orders. It is different from a halt in trading across the entire stock market. [MoneyToday report on the sidecar](https://www.mt.co.kr/stock/2026/10/13/2026101314134112327)

Foreign net flows classify investors; program flows classify trading methods. The same trade can appear in both categories, so adding the two selling totals would double-count. To determine whether futures selling led to cash-basket selling, we would need arbitrage/non-arbitrage classifications, futures-cash price differences at the same time, and open interest.

Closing data on a consistent basis were unavailable to confirm a late-session reduction in foreign futures selling. It cannot serve as confirmed evidence for a rebound. A VKOSPI reading of 48.53 is also a price for expected volatility; it does not show the direction of dealer positions.

October 13 was not the usual expiry date for October monthly options. That does not mean options hedging had no effect. Strike-level positions and option-price data are unavailable, so the contribution from gamma hedging, leveraged liquidation, or margin calls remains unverified.

## CXMT's 4F² announcement does not prove a cost advantage

CXMT is China's ChangXin Memory Technologies. It is distinct from South Korea-listed CMTX. The reported announcement was a plan to unveil a server DDR5 RDIMM using a vertical-transistor 4F² cell by year-end. CXMT disclosed the product category and target date, but not speed, capacity, yield, shipment volume, or customer qualification. [Seoul Economic Daily report on CXMT](https://www.sedaily.com/article/20101179)

Samsung Electronics also presented research on 4F² DRAM using vertical cell transistors and wafer-to-wafer hybrid copper bonding in the official ISSCC 2026 program. A research presentation, product announcement, customer qualification, and economically viable volume production are different stages. We cannot establish a two-year technology gap by placing CXMT's year-end product plan beside press reports about a Samsung production target. [Official ISSCC program, paper 15.10](https://submissions.mirasmart.com/ISSCC2026/PDF/ISSCC2026AdvanceProgram.pdf)

Samsung's 2028 production date was a target in some reports. Follow-up reporting also said that there is no confirmed timeline. CXMT, too, has stated a target rather than demonstrated commercial volume. The gap between an announcement and stable mass production remains unverified. [Tom's Hardware technology review](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/dram/cxmt-president-says-companys-first-4f2-dram-will-be-a-server-ddr5-rdimm-due-by-year-end-vertical-transistors-cut-each-memory-cells-footprint-by-a-third-on-paper-but-no-speeds-or-capacities-yet)

In 4F², F denotes a minimum feature size. Assuming the same F, cell area is 33.3% smaller than 6F² and theoretical cell-array density is 1.5 times higher. This does not mean that the cost of the entire chip, including peripheral circuits and bonding, falls by 33.3%.

The difference becomes clear with a numerical example. Assume manufacturing cost is 1.3 times the baseline, bit count is 1.5 times higher, and yield falls from 90% to 75%. The cost per good bit is then 1.3 ÷ 1.5 × (0.90 ÷ 0.75) = 1.04 times the baseline. Cost per good bit is 4% higher. The break-even yield under these assumptions is 78%. This is an illustration of why the announcement alone cannot establish the economics; it is not an estimate of CXMT's actual cost or yield.

The potential channels for Samsung should be separated by time horizon.

| Channel | Current assessment | Evidence that could change the assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Near-term share-price multiple | Competition concerns may lower expectations for the duration of earnings | Customer, supply, and pricing data that strengthen or weaken the narrative |
| Direct 2026–27 earnings impact | Public information does not support estimating the amount or timing of a decline | Actual shipments, qualification, yield, and selling prices |
| Chinese substitution for commodity DRAM imports in 2027–28 | A conditional risk, dependent on production and customer adoption | Adoption by Chinese server makers and DDR5 contract prices |
| HBM impact | This DDR5 announcement alone does not demonstrate HBM competitiveness | Separate evidence on HBM qualification, shipments, and performance |

Stronger competition in commodity DRAM could weigh on Samsung. However, comparing the earnings sensitivity of Samsung and SK hynix requires current product-level revenue and profit mix. Kioxia's decline also does not prove that CXMT is replacing NAND.

The strongest counterargument is that Chinese producers have already become commercial competitors in commodity DRAM. Uncertainty around 4F² is not a reason to ignore supply growth from existing processes. Track the success of the new process separately from volume growth in existing products. Industry caution on cost and yield is also reflected in [Fnnews' technology report](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202610131602240594).

## Assess technical rebounds and trend reversals separately

Samsung's close was (KRW 232,000 ÷ KRW 228,000 − 1) × 100 = 1.75% above its intraday low. The KOSPI also closed 2.10% above its low. This shows that some intraday selling was absorbed. It does not guarantee that buyers will hold the line the next day.

In the author's subjective assessment, the probability of a technical rebound over one to three sessions is 55–60%; the probability of a trend reversal within two weeks accompanied by foreign net buying is 25–30%. These are not outputs from a statistically validated model and are not a verified hit rate. The 55–60% estimate is also not the probability of a sustained rally.

KRW 240,000–245,000 is approximately 3.45–5.60% above the October 13 close. It is an observation range in a technical-rebound scenario, not a price target. The annual P/E estimate that could not be verified and the RSI figure without an official calculation are excluded from this assessment.

| Subsequent combination | Scenario | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Cash-equity selling slows, volatility falls, and the low holds | Technical rebound | Watch KRW 240,000–245,000; this is separate from a trend reversal |
| Price rebounds, then retests around KRW 228,000 | Base W-shaped assumption | The closing price and cash-equity flows during the retest matter |
| Close below KRW 228,000 while selling continues | Downside risk | KRW 220,000 is another level to monitor, not confirmed support |
| Foreign investors keep buying cash equities and sector concerns ease | Potential trend reversal | Flow and earnings expectations need to improve together, beyond a one-day gain |

Continued weakness in U.S. memory stocks and larger losses on individual leveraged positions could delay a rebound. The U.S. close on October 13 was not confirmed at the time of writing. Intraday declines are not presented as closing prices. Margin balances, forced selling, and options positions were also unavailable.

Conversely, the competitive narrative could weaken if results show sustained AI customer demand and funding for investment, and if DDR5 contract prices hold better than feared. Samsung's company notice set its third-quarter results announcement for 10:00 a.m. on October 29. TSMC's earnings date and U.S. inflation data matter, but this article does not present schedules or consensus estimates that it could not recheck as confirmed values. [Samsung IR notice](https://www.samsung.com/sec/ir/reports-disclosures/notices/)

## After a rebound, watch for a change in who is selling

Two consecutive sessions of net foreign buying in Samsung cash equities would strengthen the trend-reversal case. This is an observation condition to allow for confirmation, not a statistically validated trading rule. Even if VKOSPI falls below 40, the assessment should remain cautious while cash-equity selling continues.

A close that holds after a retest of KRW 228,000 is a price-based condition. If the won and government bonds weaken together and credit spreads widen, the analysis should expand to include country and credit risk. A single threshold such as USD/KRW 1,360 does not by itself confirm a shift to country risk.

On the business side, first verify the specifications, customer qualification, commercial volume, and DDR5 contract prices for CXMT's server product. These data can distinguish actual pressure on earnings from a share-price multiple contraction driven by fear. The evidence is insufficient to chase an opening-price gain without improved flows.
