Samyang Foods, the maker of Buldak Fire Noodles, reported third-quarter operating profit of <strong>₩196.2 billion</strong> on November 6. That is a record, up 49.9% from a year earlier and 6.2% above the ₩184.7 billion consensus.

Then comes the twist. The company's own presentation says only that the quarter includes "tariff refunds and other non-recurring income," and it gives no amount. A reconstruction of the earnings call by Umbrella Research puts the refund at ₩20 billion, the same size as a KB Securities preview estimate from October. Take it out and operating profit is <strong>₩176.2 billion</strong>. That is 4.6% below consensus and exactly equal to the second quarter. Revenue missed by 2.6%, and net profit missed by 36%.

So is the fire in the demand, or in the accounting? Our reading is that the demand is real and the headline beat is seasoning. The extra spice came once, and it will not be in the fourth quarter.

<strong>Our view: Buldak demand is not the problem, and the headline beat is not the news. Underlying operating profit was flat on the second quarter at ₩176.2 billion. The next quarter loses the refund and meets a stronger won, so estimates are more likely to fall, starting with net profit. Profit growth in 2027 depends on supply: the Jiaxing plant in China starts mass production in January 2027, and management mentioned country-by-country price increases for the first time. Tourists matter far less than the headlines suggest.</strong>

- Reported operating profit beat consensus by 6.2%. Without the estimated refund it missed by 4.6% and was flat on the second quarter.
- U.S. subsidiary revenue grew about 10% on the quarter and about 33% on the year in dollars. The strong won hides part of that in the won figures.
- Gross margin fell about 4 points, and cuts in freight and advertising filled the gap. Net profit missed by 36% because of ₩68.8 billion in non-operating losses, mostly unrealized currency losses.
- Miryang plants 1 and 2 run at 82.3% utilization, so supply sets the pace of growth until Jiaxing starts. Samyang has announced no expansion after Jiaxing.
- Tourists buy about ₩11 billion to ₩51 billion of Samyang products a year in Korea, 0.4% to 1.6% of revenue. The regional pattern gives no sign that visitors turn into sales abroad.

## The extra spice was a one-time ₩20 billion

How does a quarter with flat underlying profit print ₩20 billion more? We split the change from the second quarter into four steps. The split uses the cost-of-sales basis of Umbrella Research's call reconstruction.

![Where the extra spice sits. Operating profit bridge from the second to the third quarter of 2026, ₩ billion, axis starting at 100. Second quarter 176.2, revenue growth plus 10.3, gross margin drop minus 31.8 (48.1% to 44.1%), SG&A cuts plus 21.6 (freight 11.0, advertising 7.9, other 2.7), tariff refund estimate plus 20.0, third quarter reported 196.2. The steps sum to 196.3 because of rounding.](/post-assets/buldak-fire-noodles-samyang-q3-beat-was-tariff-refund-2026-11-08/bridge.en.svg)

Revenue growth added ₩10.3 billion. Without the refund, the gross margin fell from 48.1% to 44.1% and took away ₩31.8 billion. The company points to palm oil and other input costs, oil and LNG energy costs, and the stronger won. It did not say how much each one cost. Lower SG&A gave back ₩21.6 billion: freight ₩11.0 billion, advertising ₩7.9 billion and other ₩2.7 billion. The refund added the last ₩20.0 billion.

Read it as a cost squeeze that savings and a one-time refund covered. That reading depends on where the refund sits.

| If the refund sits in | Gross margin | SG&A | The story |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost of sales (Umbrella Research) | 44.1% without the refund, down 4.0 points | down ₩21.6B on the quarter | A large cost squeeze, covered by freight and advertising savings |
| SG&A (wording in the company deck) | 46.6%, down 1.5 points | ₩192.8B without the refund, 24.4% of sales, no savings | A small cost squeeze and no savings |

Umbrella Research explains the lower SG&A through freight and advertising, so we use the cost-of-sales basis as our default. Both readings give the same operating profit. They differ only in the cost story.

Against a year earlier, operating profit rose ₩65.3 billion, and most of that is sales growth. Last year's cost ratio of 58.7% was the highest of the seven quarters we compared, so the base was easy.

| Change in operating profit from 3Q25 (₩130.9B to ₩196.2B) | Amount | Arithmetic |
|---|---:|---|
| Revenue growth at last year's 41.3% gross margin | +₩66.0B | ₩159.8B × 41.3% |
| Gross margin from 41.3% to 44.1% (without refund) | +₩21.8B | |
| Tariff refund (estimate) | +₩20.0B | second-hand figure |
| Higher SG&A | −₩42.5B | ₩172.8B − ₩130.3B |

## The demand is still hot, and the strong won hides part of it

![Heat by region. Third-quarter 2026 revenue growth, year on year, in won. Europe plus 65.5% (₩86.9 billion), Japan plus 39.0% (₩11.1 billion), United States plus 36.6% (₩212.5 billion, about plus 33% in dollars), Other plus 19.5% (₩123.7 billion), China plus 13.2% (₩208.9 billion), Korea plus 7.9% (₩131.1 billion), Indonesia minus 20.8% (₩5.1 billion). All overseas plus 29.4% (₩660.8 billion, 83% of sales).](/post-assets/buldak-fire-noodles-samyang-q3-beat-was-tariff-refund-2026-11-08/regions.en.svg)

Samyang sold ₩660.8 billion abroad, about 83% of revenue and up 29.4% on the year. Europe grew 65.5%, Japan 39.0% and the United States 36.6% in won. China grew 13.2% and Korea 7.9%. Indonesia fell 20.8% after the Lebaran holiday season and weaker consumer spending.

The company deck and Umbrella Research give these drivers. In the United States, Walmart grew 26.1% on the quarter, mainstream channels make up 64% of sales, and Mexico is part of the story. In China, shipments ahead of the Mid-Autumn Festival and Singles' Day helped. Europe expanded in the Netherlands, Germany and Poland while accounts in France and Italy were swapped. In Japan, convenience-store vendor sales held up.

![The strong won hides part of the heat. U.S. subsidiary revenue growth, percent. Quarter on quarter: plus 4.4% in won, about plus 10% in dollars. Year on year: plus 36.6% in won, about plus 33% in dollars. Average USD/KRW was 1,385 in 3Q25, 1,500 in 2Q26 and 1,420 in 3Q26.](/post-assets/buldak-fire-noodles-samyang-q3-beat-was-tariff-refund-2026-11-08/fxhide.en.svg)

The won makes the U.S. look slower than it is. In won, U.S. revenue rose 4.4% on the quarter. The average rate moved from 1,500 to 1,420 won per dollar, so in dollars the growth was about 10%. On the year, the effect runs the other way: +36.6% in won and about +33% in dollars. The dollar figures are our calculation from won revenue and average rates.

Revenue came in ₩21.0 billion below consensus. The company's explanations are account changes in Europe and the United Kingdom, ₩10 billion to ₩11 billion of shipments that left port late and move into the fourth quarter, and an average exchange rate 5.3% lower.

## Net profit took the burn

Net profit missed consensus by ₩50.2 billion because of ₩68.8 billion in non-operating losses (pre-tax profit of ₩127.4 billion against operating profit of ₩196.2 billion). Umbrella Research puts the currency-related loss at ₩68.1 billion. The company says most of it is unrealized valuation loss on foreign-currency assets, caused by the stronger won.

The won averaged 1,420 per dollar in the third quarter after 1,500 in the second. On November 6 it stood at 1,356, close to the 1,350.5 at the end of September. So little extra valuation loss has built up so far in the fourth quarter.

## Next quarter loses the seasoning packet, and 2027 depends on supply

![Next quarter loses the seasoning packet. Quarterly operating profit, ₩ billion. Third quarter 2025 130.9. Second quarter 2026 176.2. Third quarter 2026 196.2, made of 176.2 underlying plus 20.0 refund estimate. Fourth quarter 2026 estimate range 155 to 180 with a center near 160. The consensus-implied fourth quarter is 177.2.](/post-assets/buldak-fire-noodles-samyang-q3-beat-was-tariff-refund-2026-11-08/quarters.en.svg)

A 24.8% operating margin will not last. Without the refund the margin was about 22%, and it holds only if price increases cover the stronger won and higher costs. The next two quarters belong to the exchange rate and costs. 2027 belongs to supply.

Our range for fourth-quarter operating profit is ₩155 billion to ₩180 billion, with a center near ₩160 billion. It is a subjective estimate. The consensus implies ₩177.2 billion, which is ₩726.7 billion for 2026 less ₩549.5 billion for the first three quarters. We start from ₩176.2 billion without the refund.

| Step | ₩ billion | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| Start: 3Q26 without refund | 176.2 | |
| Currency: USD/KRW has averaged 1,338 so far in 4Q, 5.8% below the 3Q average of 1,420 | −15 (range −4 to −25) | Hana Securities' sensitivity of ₩19B a year per 100 won gives about −4. Translating overseas sales gives about −25 |
| Europe: shipments deferred from 3Q | +2.5 | |
| United States growth | +3.0 | |
| China seasonal decline | −8.0 | China revenue fell 19.7% on the quarter in 4Q25 |
| Result | about 160 (155 to 180) | Seasonally higher SG&A is a downside risk (SG&A ratio was 23.9% in 4Q25) |

The consensus-implied ₩177.2 billion sits about 10% above our center, so we expect estimates to be cut. The refund disappears and the won stays strong, so the cut comes first in net profit.

What would change our reading:

- Down: U.S. subsidiary revenue shrinks in dollars on the quarter, volume falls after a price increase, or Jiaxing slips past the second quarter of 2027.
- Up: price increases in the United States and Europe in 1Q27 with volume held, or an announcement of the next overseas plant.
- Our test for 4Q26: operating margin without the refund of 22% or more keeps our view. Below 21%, we revisit margins.

| Variable | Now | 2027 | Our reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| Demand | U.S. up about 10% on the quarter in dollars, Europe up 65.5% on the year | Holds | Strong, and not the constraint today |
| Supply | Miryang plants 1 and 2 at 82.3% utilization | Jiaxing starts mass production in January 2027 (8 lines, 1.13 billion packs a year, about +40% of supply per the call reconstruction) | The dominant variable for 2027 growth |
| Price | First official mention of country-by-country price increases on the call. Japan retail price up about 13% in October (Hana) | U.S. and Europe increases possible in 1Q27, together with the raw-material renewal | A margin defense |
| Costs | Palm oil and energy up | Raw-material contracts renew in 1Q27, prices likely higher | Price increases have to offset it |
| Exchange rate | 3Q average 1,420 | 4Q average so far 1,338 | The main swing for the next two quarters |
| One-offs | Refund +₩20B, non-operating loss −₩68.8B (mostly currency valuation) | Refund gone | Lowers the base for 4Q operating profit |
| Tariffs | Refund linked to U.S. IEEPA tariffs | U.S. base tariff about 10% (secondary source), litigation continues | Further refunds are an option, not a base case |
| Next expansion | None announced after Jiaxing | | A risk for 2028 and after |

Profit growth should continue through 2027, because demand exceeds supply and Jiaxing adds supply. After 2028 it depends on the next expansion decision. Umbrella Research puts construction at about three years, so a site chosen now would not start until 2029 or later.

## Do tourists eat enough Buldak to matter? Not for the income statement

Korea welcomed 17.11 million visitors from January to September, up 21.5%. The 2025 full-year record of 18.94 million fell on October 27. Foreign card spending reached ₩15.9 trillion, up 47.7%. By nationality, China grew 29.2% (5.47 million visitors), Japan 16.9%, Taiwan 32.4%, the Americas 12.0% and Europe 20.0%. These figures come from Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism releases as reported in the press.

It is tempting to read that as a Buldak story: visitors buy a pack in Seoul, go home and keep buying. We could size the first half. We found no evidence for the second.

![If Samyang's year were 100 bowls of Buldak. Direct sales to tourists in Korea are 0.4 to 1.6 bowls out of 100, which is ₩11 billion to ₩51 billion a year, or 0.4% to 1.6% of about ₩3.07 trillion. This year's 21.5% jump in visitors adds 0.07 to 0.3 bowls, which is ₩2 billion to ₩9 billion a year.](/post-assets/buldak-fire-noodles-samyang-q3-beat-was-tariff-refund-2026-11-08/bowls.en.svg)

| Assumption | Low | High |
|---|---:|---:|
| Visitors in 2026 (January to September annualized) | 22.8 million | 22.8 million |
| Share buying groceries (2Q26 foreign tourist survey) | 56.9% | 56.9% |
| Ramen buyers among grocery buyers (our assumption) | 40% | 60% |
| Samyang share of ramen bought (our assumption) | 35% | 50% |
| Samyang spend per person at retail prices (our assumption) | ₩10,000 | ₩20,000 |
| Ex-factory price as a share of retail (our assumption) | 60% | 65% |
| Samyang revenue | about ₩11B a year | about ₩51B a year |
| Share of 2026 revenue (about ₩3.07 trillion) | 0.4% | 1.6% |

The 21.5% increase in visitors adds ₩2 billion to ₩9 billion of revenue a year. At the domestic margin, that is a couple of billion won of operating profit at most. The results point the same way. Domestic revenue growth slowed from 23.2% in 1Q26 to 10.3% in 2Q26 and 7.9% in 3Q26, while visitor growth stayed above 20%. The company credits a new product for domestic growth in the third quarter.

The indirect path may exist: visitors try Buldak in Korea and buy it back home. We found no survey that measures it. The regional comparison argues against a strong link.

![Tourists point one way, Buldak sales the other. Growth by region, year on year, percent. China: visitors plus 29.2%, Samyang sales plus 13.2%. Japan: visitors plus 16.9%, Samyang sales plus 39.0%. Americas versus Samyang U.S.: visitors plus 12.0%, Samyang sales plus 36.6%. Europe: visitors plus 20.0%, Samyang sales plus 65.5%. Visitors are January to September, Samyang is the third quarter in won.](/post-assets/buldak-fire-noodles-samyang-q3-beat-was-tariff-refund-2026-11-08/tourists.en.svg)

China sends the fastest-growing group of visitors (+29.2%), yet Samyang's China revenue grew only 13.2%, among the slowest. The Americas (+12.0%) and Europe (+20.0%) send slower-growing groups, and Samyang's sales there grew 36.6% and 65.5%. Walmart and other mainstream channels, together with a change of major accounts, explain the growth in the United States and Europe.

We read visitor numbers and Buldak demand as two results of one cause, the spread of K-content. Visitor numbers are a coincident indicator for Buldak, not a leading one. Inbound tourism shows up more directly in the income statements of convenience stores, duty-free shops, cosmetics retailers, and hotels and casinos. Samyang is a weak proxy for it.

## Data notes

- We did not obtain a transcript of the call. The ₩20 billion refund, the 82.3% utilization, the price-increase remark, and the freight and advertising savings come from one article by Umbrella Research, a paid independent research house, reconstructing the call (Alpha Biz, November 7). The ₩20 billion matches a KB Securities preview estimate of about ₩20 billion. The company's own deck gives no refund amount.
- The call reconstruction puts Jiaxing at 1.13 billion packs a year and about 40% more supply. It also gives 1.58 billion packs a year for Miryang plants 1 and 2, and Jiaxing would be about 70% of that figure. So the base of the 40% probably includes other capacity. We have not seen the breakdown.
- Broker reviews written after the results were not yet indexed on November 8.
- Results are preliminary and have not had the outside auditor's review.
- Consensus is Naver Finance (FnGuide source) as of November 5. We could not query FnGuide directly. Some pre-results articles cited ₩186.8 billion for operating profit.
- The company does not split the currency effect, so the currency step in our fourth-quarter range is a wide assumption.
- Visitor figures are press reports of Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism releases. We did not open the Korea Tourism Data Lab tables. The ramen share, Samyang share and spend in the tourist table are assumptions.
- The U.S. tariff rate comes from a tariff-consulting website and was not checked against the Federal Register.
- The share price is the KRX regular-session close on November 6, and the after-hours price is the Nextrade close at 20:00. The fourth-quarter range, the 2026 estimate and the multiples are our subjective estimates.
