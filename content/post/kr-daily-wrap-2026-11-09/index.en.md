## Market Wrap

*Source: KR Market Snapshot, Nov 9. No full close briefing available; same-day DB used.*

Korea's market closed in soft territory. Of 2,599 listed names, 1,076 advanced and 1,274 declined — a slightly negative breadth count — but the average price change was +0.19%, signaling that gains concentrated in a handful of larger names masked broader selling.

**Battery and EV complex under coordinated pressure.** The session's loudest signal came from the battery chain. Samsung SDI (006400.KS) dropped –11.32% with both foreign and institutional investors selling simultaneously. LG Energy Solution (373220.KS) fell –5.78%, Eco Pro BM (247540.KQ) –8.66%, and Eco Pro (086520.KS) –8.46%. This is a cross-sector flush, not random noise, and aligns with global EV demand softness while WTI holds near $100.

**Pharma and biotech diverged.** SK Bioscience (302440.KS) rose +5.21% on foreign buying. Samsung Biologics (207940.KS) edged down –0.40% but drew net institutional accumulation — a stabilizing pattern worth watching. The sector is bifurcated: CDMO and vaccine platforms attracting inflows, legacy names under pressure.

**IT services showed selective strength.** LG CNS (064400.KS) gained +4.32% with two-sided institutional and foreign buying — genuine shared conviction, not retail momentum. NAVER (035420.KS) slipped –1.63% but institutions were net buyers, pointing to quiet accumulation into weakness.

**Airlines flagged by consensus upgrades.** Jeju Air (089590.KS) and Jin Air (272450.KS) both held their prices while sell-side analysts revised estimates higher — a setup where the market has not yet caught up to improving fundamentals.

**Cosmetics sold off broadly.** Cosmax (192820.KS) –5.30% and Cosméka Korea (241710.KS) –15.09% saw coordinated foreign and institutional selling. The K-beauty complex faces a rough session on cross-border demand concerns.

Overall character: **selective rotation**. Capital moved away from batteries and cosmetics toward pharma CDMOs and IT services. Breadth was mildly negative, but several quality names are being quietly accumulated beneath the surface.

### Top 3 in Detail

**1. Hanmi Pharmaceutical (128940.KS)**
Korea's leading innovative pharma company, with biologics in diabetes/obesity, oncology, and rare diseases. It hits four screener families: Consensus Up, Cycle Rerating, Smart Money Earnings, and Smart Money Quality. Retail sellers offloaded approximately ₩64.5bn over the measurement period; foreign and quality-institutional buyers absorbed that supply — a classic divergence between weaker and stronger hands. Operating margin expanded +2.2pp. DART shows an active IR calendar and a confirmed licensing deal for LAGLA (Fabry disease treatment) filed Oct 30. **Next check**: sell-side consensus revision size, LAGLA deal economics, and whether absorption holds this week.

> **Why ranked above Dongkuk despite lower raw score**: the Consensus Up family carries a 2.20× historical weight, well above the 0.58× applied to financial factor screeners. Hanmi's estimate revision signal has stronger demonstrated forward-return lift in back-testing than a pure fundamental quality stack.

**2. Nanoscale Materials (121600.KQ)**
Specialty materials supplier for semiconductor and display manufacturing. It appears in both Cycle Rerating and Consensus Up. The treasury share disposal filed Nov 6 is a capital allocation signal that often precedes positive management guidance. The consensus z-score is modest (+0.65) but directionally positive. **Next check**: disposal structure (employee compensation vs. open-market sale), customer concentration in HBM or CXL-related substrate demand, and whether the cycle rerating is a leading or lagging read.

**3. Dongkuk Pharmaceutical (086450.KS)**
Domestic mid-cap pharma with stable consumer health and specialty drug divisions. The broadest fundamental case in today's list: Quality Compounder, Cycle Rerating, PEAD, and Quiet Accumulation all trigger together. ROE 9.9%, OP YoY +20.1%, retail exit absorbed by institutional buyers. The PEAD screener places it in Tier C with a 52-week gap of 21.9% and price above MA50 — drift space remains open. Note: PEAD financial data is 242 days old (annual filing), so the most recent quarterly print needs independent verification. **Next check**: Q3 2026 quarterly results, which product line is driving OP acceleration, and whether the quiet accumulation pattern precedes a disclosed catalyst.
