**Dongkook Pharmaceutical (086450.KS)** is a Korean drugmaker. It leads because four screener families flag it: Quality Compounder, Cycle Rerating, PEAD and Quiet Accumulation. Foreign and quality-institution buyers absorbed about 4.9 billion won of individual selling. The only recent DART filing is a shareholder meeting notice, so no company-specific catalyst is visible. The financials come from the 2025 annual filing, which is 245 days old. The next check is the latest quarterly report, to confirm the margin trend still holds.

**TYM (002900.KS)** makes agricultural machinery. Its case rests on estimate upgrades and official events. The company filed a treasury share buyback decision on Nov 11 and preliminary earnings on Nov 4. The PEAD screen shows operating profit up about 298% year on year, but that figure comes from a snapshot source with no filing date. It is a growth filter, not a consensus surprise. Check the earnings release and the buyback size.

**Celltrion (068270.KS)** is a biosimilar maker. It is flagged for margin expansion (+14.2pp), PEAD and RS80 leadership, and about 100 billion won of individual selling was absorbed by foreign and quality-institution buyers. DART shows a catalyst filing on Nov 2: a European Phase 3 trial plan for its CTP13 subcutaneous product in rheumatoid arthritis. The financial data is also from the 2025 annual filing. The next check is the Q3 numbers and the trial timeline.

Nano Advanced Materials (121600.KQ) has the strongest money-flow evidence outside the top three. It also filed a share cancellation decision today. Korean Air (003490.KS) and Samah Aluminum (006110.KS) appear in the full ranking, with consensus upgrades as their only screen signal.

All names are screened candidates for further research, not recommendations. KT Genie Music (043610.KS) carries one DART risk-type filing and is excluded from the top table.
