## Market Wrap

Monday was a broad risk-off session for the Korea stock market. KOSPI closed near 5,100, down 2.65% over the five-day window. Of 2,596 stocks, 495 rose, 1,862 fell and 239 were unchanged. The advance-decline proxy was 26.6, and the average stock fell 2.11%. KOSDAQ held up better than KOSPI over the window, down only 0.29%.

Semiconductor equipment and secondary-battery names took the heaviest losses. PSK Holdings (031980.KQ) fell 12.95% and Hana Micron (067310.KQ) fell 15.72%. Tes (095610.KQ) dropped 11.56% and DB HiTek (000990.KS) lost 8.95%. Hanwha Solutions (009830.KS) fell 6.34% and Samsung SDI (006400.KS) fell 3.24%. Several biotech names also sold off, including LigaChem Biosciences (141080.KQ) at -10.93% and Olix (226950.KQ) at -7.29%.

Financials and consumer names held up better. Woori Financial (316140.KS) rose 1.54%, Shinhan Financial (055550.KS) rose 1.00% and Samsung Fire & Marine (000810.KS) gained 2.46%. Samyang Foods (003230.KS) rose 6.20%, and Hanjin KAL (180640.KS) rose 5.90%.

Flow signals were mixed. Foreigners were net buyers of Samyang Foods, APR (278470.KS), Hanjin KAL and HMM (011200.KS). They were net sellers of Samsung SDI, Woori Financial, KT&G (033780.KS) and Shinhan Financial. Institutions bought financials and autos, led by Woori Financial, Samsung Fire & Marine and Hyundai Motor (005380.KS). They sold Samsung SDI, PSK Holdings and DB HiTek. The snapshot does not report net-buy amounts or turnover, so size is unverified.

Derivatives and passive flows were negative. The market-wide program flow was -641.2 billion won, with non-arbitrage at -551.2 billion won. Futures basis was -2.21 and ETF net creation was -223.6 billion won (as of Nov 13). Open interest rose by 3,128 contracts to 55,982. The program selling is below the model's -1.5 trillion won trigger. A dollar move also matters: the dollar index rose to 103.5 while the won strengthened slightly.
