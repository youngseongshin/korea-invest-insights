## Market Wrap

November 11 was a broad, high-conviction advance. Of 2,593 tracked stocks, **1,670 gained, 680 fell, 243 were unchanged** — a 71% advance rate. The ADR proxy printed 245.6 and the average stock returned +1.81%. This is not an index-driven headline move; participation was genuine and wide.

**Themes that led:**

Samsung SDI (006400.KS, +4.93%) attracted both foreign and institutional buyers simultaneously — the cleanest two-sided confirmation in the space today.

**Semiconductor back-end and PCB** saw explosive moves with institutional support. Korea Circuit (007810.KS) jumped +25.58% on institutional buying. TSI (131290.KQ, +23.24%) also attracted institutional flows, but foreign investors were net sellers — a split-flow reading that warrants caution before extrapolating.

**Pharma and bio** featured strong institutional demand. Hanmi Pharmaceutical (128940.KS, +5.27%), Ligachem Biosciences (141080.KQ, +10.79%), and ABL Bio (298380.KQ, +13.48%) all recorded institutional net buying.

**Weak pockets:**

Insurance was the most notable sector under pressure. Samsung Fire (000810.KS, −5.51%), Hyundai Marine (001450.KS, −3.00%), and DB Insurance (005830.KS, −1.63%) declined with institutional selling. Lunit (328130.KQ, −16.03%) was the session's sharpest institutional net sell. Emart (139480.KS, −8.05%) and Korea Zinc (010130.KS, −4.96%) also underperformed.

**Flow character:** The dominant pattern today was retail supply absorbed by foreign and institutional buyers across multiple names and sectors — constructive distribution dynamics in a broad risk-on session.
