### Top 3 Deep Context

**1. Seegene (096530.KQ) — Quality + Smart Money + Cycle: the full three-layer signal**

Seegene is a molecular diagnostics company, best known globally for its COVID-era PCR kits. Post-pandemic, the narrative had collapsed — and that's the re-rating setup. Operating profit is up 309.7% year-over-year, and operating margins have expanded by 11.3 percentage points, suggesting the cost structure has been reset while revenue is recovering. The smart money flow data sharpens the story: foreign and quality institutional investors absorbed ₩21.5 billion in net retail supply over the past five days (retail: −₩21.7B). That is exactly the flow pattern that precedes a market re-pricing event — insiders and professionals accumulating while retail distributes. A DART catalyst filing (dividend record date) dated Sep 28 adds a near-term event flag. What to verify next: whether the OP recovery is durable beyond base effects from the COVID trough, and whether the margin improvement reflects structural cost discipline or one-time items.

**2. Samsung Electronics (005930.KS) — Maximum evidence, maximum tension**

Samsung scores the highest composite family score (67.9) with seven independent evidence families: earnings cycle, PEAD drift, fundamental quality, investor flow, market activity, official filings, and price leadership. The earnings fundamentals are intact — ROE 10.7%, operating profit +33.2% YoY, margin expansion of +2.2pp, PEAD score confirms post-earnings drift signal. But the flow data creates a clear caution: foreigners and quality institutions net sold ₩1.829 trillion over five days, with individual investors absorbing the supply (+₩295.9 billion). This is a contested name. The re-rating thesis is structurally valid; the timing question is whether institutional distribution has run its course. It belongs in a watchlist alongside the flow data rather than as a conviction entry point today. DART filings (Oct 2: major shareholder changes) are routine.

**3. Pan Ocean (028670.KS) — Smart money with a contract catalyst**

Pan Ocean is one of Korea's major dry bulk shipping companies. It scores on Smart Money Quality and Smart Money Earnings simultaneously — foreign and quality institutional investors net bought ₩16.2 billion over five days while retail sold ₩15.2 billion. The RS80 screen classifies it as a "Focus Candidate" with a pivot gap of +2.3% and 20-day supply/demand ratio running positive (+6.01%). Two DART catalyst filings in the past 10 days (single sales/supply contracts, Oct 1 and Sep 28) confirm operational news flow. The setup here is a business with improving earnings generating real smart-money accumulation backed by public contract announcements. What to verify: the specific contract values relative to Pan Ocean's revenue base, and whether dry bulk rate trends support margin continuation.
