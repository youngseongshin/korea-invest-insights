Today's session was broadly negative beneath the surface. Of 2,593 names tracked, 1,793 declined against 542 advances. The ADR proxy came in at 30.2 and the average stock return hit −1.59% — the kind of internal weakness that makes the KOSPI headline a poor guide to what actually happened.

**Financials and data infrastructure attracted buyers.** KB금융 (105560.KS) +1.82% and 삼성화재해상보험 (000810.KS) +2.20% were foreign net-buy names. Domestically, institutions accumulated 삼성SDI (006400.KS), 삼성생명 (032830.KS), 신한지주 (055550.KS), and 현대모비스 (012330.KS). 한화비전 (489790.KS) surged 22.14% on institutional buying against foreign selling — the day's strongest single-name momentum. 케이아이엔엑스 (093320.KQ) +13.28% was another institutional pick in the data infrastructure theme.

**Passive flow context:** Program trading was a modest net +₩120bn; ETF net creation reached +₩1,151bn — a large inflow. But basis sits at −0.87, below the +1.0 threshold for passive-confirmed support. Large ETF inflows with negative basis suggest asset allocation rebalancing rather than directional conviction.

The session's character: selective risk-off, with institutional rotation out of construction, batteries, and renewables into financials and infrastructure. In a Bear regime where only one in five stocks is above its 50-DMA, quality and earnings momentum are the few filters that matter.

### Deep-Dives: Top 3

**1. Daedeok Electronics (대덕전자, 353200.KQ) — Operating Leverage Meets Official Catalyst**

Daedeok is a PCB substrate manufacturer in Korea's semiconductor packaging supply chain. Five independent evidence families at a meta score of 57.2 place it at the top of today's queue. The operating leverage story is blunt: OP YoY +336%, margin expansion +3.4pp, with ROE rebuilding from a low base to 5.3%. The stock sits 27.6% below its 52-week high and 14.9% above the 50-DMA, leaving drift room intact. A DART IR event was filed Oct 23 — the catalyst layer is present. The caution is meaningful: net foreign+institutional selling of −₩6.3bn over five days signals sophisticated holders using strength to exit. The key question is whether the IR event reverses that flow or accelerates it.

**2. TFE (티에프이, 425420.KQ) — All Four Layers, Institutional Absorption**

TFE makes semiconductor test handler equipment. This is the only name today with the full four-layer stack — Quality Compounder, Smart Money Quality, Smart Money Earnings, and Cycle Rerating — which is the most complete signal structure the framework can produce. The fundamentals confirm it: ROE 16.6%, OP YoY +334%, and margin expansion of +11.1pp indicate a business deep in an earnings inflection. The flow pattern is equally clear: foreign and qualified institutional investors bought a net +₩14.3bn over five days while retail sold −₩15.8bn. That's institutional accumulation against retail distribution — one of the framework's cleanest money-flow setups. A large-holding disclosure was filed at DART on Oct 23. Next check: whether the five-day +₩14.3bn buying pace continues or was a block repositioning.

**3. Dongkuk Pharmaceutical (동국제약, 086450.KQ) — Defensive Compounder in a Risk-Off Session**

Dongkuk Pharmaceutical is a domestic pharma and nutraceutical company with a long record of steady compounding. It scores on five evidence families including fundamental quality, cycle rerating, and post-earnings drift. OP YoY at +20% is the lowest of the top five — but on a healthcare platform with recurring revenues, consistent margin expansion (+0.5pp) and ROE of 9.9% make it a defensive quality candidate. Foreign and institutional investors absorbed retail selling of −₩6.2bn, suggesting quiet accumulation. A shareholder meeting has been called (DART Oct 12); if strategic restructuring is on the agenda, the catalyst layer strengthens. In a session where construction and batteries are being sold indiscriminately, healthcare compounders with earnings improvement are a logical destination for repositioning capital.
