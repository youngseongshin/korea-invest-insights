## Market Wrap

*Source: KR Market Snapshot, 2026-11-06. No same-day close briefing was available; a stale prior briefing was intentionally blocked.*

Today's session was a broad, largely indiscriminate selloff. Of 2,595 stocks tracked, 2,035 declined and only 335 advanced — an advance-decline ratio of roughly 1:6. The average stock fell 2.34%. Program selling (−₩1.61 trillion net, nearly all non-arbitrage) confirmed systematic de-risking rather than targeted rotation. ETF net creation was a modest +₩181 billion — passive inflows providing a marginal cushion against the tide.

**Batteries and semiconductors led the decline.** Samsung SDI (006400.KS, −8.06%), LG Chem (051910.KS, −5.69%), EcoPro (086520.KS, −6.89%), and L&F (066970.KQ, −12.60%) all fell sharply, with institutions actively selling SDI and L&F. On the semiconductor side, TES (095610.KS, −11.59%), Dooson Tesna (131970.KS, −10.85%), FADU (440110.KQ, −10.27%), and TCK (064760.KQ, −11.41%) were battered. Korea Finance Holdings (071050.KS, −17.34%) stood out as the worst single-name move, with heavy institutional selling.

**Internet and IT services were the clearest defensive pocket.** NAVER (035420.KS, +2.87%) was the only large-cap that attracted both foreign and institutional buying simultaneously. Samsung SDS (018260.KS, +6.34%) also drew institutional flows. Samsung Biologics (207940.KS, −0.26%) was nearly flat — the healthiest of the defensives among large caps.

**Foreign flow diverged from price.** Foreigners sold financials outright: KB Financial (105560.KS, −3.82%), Woori Financial (316140.KS, −1.72%), and Shinhan (055550.KS, −2.25%) all saw foreign outflows. Samsung C&T (028260.KS, −5.85%) and Samsung SDI were also on the foreign sell list. On the other side, foreigners bought POSCO Holdings (005490.KS, −3.11%) and TES despite price declines — a potential signal for recovery scenarios.

**Institutions bought financials that foreigners sold.** KB Financial, Woori, Shinhan, and Hyundai Motor (005380.KS) all appeared on institutional buy lists. The setup — foreign selling, domestic institutional absorbing — may reflect a rotation toward domestic yield plays as Korean 10-year bonds fell to 3.887%, a five-week low. Korean AA− corporate spreads over 3-year KTBs tightened to 0.69pp, one of the more stable readings in the current drawdown.
