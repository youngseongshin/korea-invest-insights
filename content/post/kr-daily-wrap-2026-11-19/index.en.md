## Market Wrap

Breadth was positive. Of 2,577 stocks, 1,394 rose, 915 fell and 268 were unchanged. The ADR proxy was 152.3 and the average stock gained 0.91%. Total turnover and the volume-confirmed gainer and decliner tables were empty in today's snapshot, so this wrap leans on breadth and flow rankings.

**Strong:** Entertainment and media led. Hybe (352820.KS) jumped 13.51% and CJ ENM (035760.KQ) rose 28.11%. SM Entertainment (041510.KQ) gained 7.85% and JYP Ent. (035900.KQ) 2.25%. Korean Air (003490.KS) added 4.15%, and HPSP (403870.KQ) rose 9.78%.

**Weak:** Energy and batteries sold off. S-Oil (010950.KS) fell 6.27% and SK Innovation (096770.KS) 6.06%. GS (078930.KS) lost 4.18%, Samsung SDI (006400.KS) 2.42% and LG Energy Solution (373220.KS) 2.13%. Hyundai Mobis (012330.KS) dropped 4.71% and Samsung SDS (018260.KS) 7.36%.

**Flows:** Foreign investors were top net buyers of Hybe, Korean Air, CJ ENM and LG Energy Solution. They were top net sellers of Hyundai Mobis, HPSP and Samsung SDS. Institutions also bought Hybe and HPSP, so those two had support from both groups. Institutions were top net sellers of SK Innovation, Samsung SDI, LG Energy Solution and S-Oil. The energy and battery weakness therefore had institutional selling behind it. Net-flow amounts were redacted in the source.

**HMM (011200.KS)** is a shipping company. It passes the quality screen, and estimates are rising. Foreign and quality-institution buyers absorbed 26.99 billion KRW of individual selling. Its filings include a third-quarter report (Nov 12) and an amended corporate value-up plan. The next check is whether earnings growth of 8.9% is enough to support further re-rating.

**Kakao Games (293490.KQ)** is a game publisher. It appears because estimates are rising and its price is leading the market, with 20-day flow at +11.49% of turnover. It also held an investor relations event on Nov 5. A Nov 16 amended filing on a stake acquisition in another company needs a read before any conclusion.

**Protec (053610.KQ)** makes semiconductor-related equipment. It is the strongest earnings story in the top three. Operating profit rose 163% year on year, revenue rose 55%, and ROE is 21.6%. The PEAD screen measures year-on-year growth, not a surprise versus consensus. Rising estimates (z +0.52) partly fill that gap. Check how much of the earnings jump is repeatable.

**Celltrion** has the clearest earnings and cycle story, with operating profit up 99%, but its ROE is 7.67%. Individuals sold 64.5 billion KRW and institutions absorbed it. Also see Samyang Foods (003230.KS) and Koses (089890.KQ), both in PEAD Tier A, though neither reached the meta top 10.

**Cautions:** Exicon's five-day net selling by foreign and quality institutions was 11.13 billion KRW, absorbed by individuals. Korea Gas Corp has one risk-type DART filing. These are screening candidates, not recommendations. Each needs verification against filings and price action.
