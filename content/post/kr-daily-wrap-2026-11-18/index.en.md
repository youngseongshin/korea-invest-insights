## Market Wrap

The KOSPI session was broad but not one-directional. Of 2,532 stocks, 1,279 rose, 997 fell and 256 were unchanged. The advance/decline ratio was 128.3, and the average stock gained 0.53%. Same-day turnover data was missing, so we cannot say how much volume backed the move. The index close is also not in the source.

**Strong themes.** Robotics led. Robotis (108490.KQ) rose 13.24% and Rainbow Robotics (277810.KQ) rose 20.06%. Both appear among top institutional net buyers, and Robotis also made the foreign net-buy list. Holding companies and insurers also did well. SK (034730.KS) gained 5.26%, Doosan (000150.KS) 8.69% and Samsung Life (032830.KS) 5.37%. Entertainment stocks rallied too, with SM Entertainment (041510.KQ) up 14.30% and Hybe (352820.KS) up 7.62%.

**Weak themes.** Batteries and some chemicals were soft. Samsung SDI (006400.KS) fell 2.36% and LG Chem (051910.KS) fell 3.03%. Both foreigners and institutions were net sellers of Samsung SDI, and institutions also sold LG Energy Solution (373220.KS), which slipped 0.16%. Samsung E&A (028050.KS) lost 5.60% with selling from both groups. Samsundangje Pharm (000250.KS) fell 14.56% as institutions sold.

**Flows.** Foreign and institutional flows pointed in different directions on several of the day's biggest movers. Doosan and SK were top institutional buys but also on the foreign net-sell list. Hyundai Motor (005380.KS), up 3.09%, was a foreign net buy. Foreigners and institutions agreed only in a few places, such as robotics. Net-flow amounts were redacted in the source.

**HMM (011200.KS)** is a container shipper. It passes the quality screen and also shows rising consensus, quiet accumulation, RS80 leadership and smart-money quality. Over five days, foreigners and quality institutions bought a net KRW 32.8bn, while individuals sold KRW 25.4bn. Recent filings include a related-party investment (Nov 17), a corporate value-up plan correction and the Q3 report. Operating profit growth of 8.9% is modest, so the case rests on consensus revisions holding. Check next whether the related-party investment changes capital allocation.

**Celltrion (068270.KS)** is a biopharma company. It shows up in the cycle re-rating, PEAD and RS80 screens. Operating profit nearly doubled (+99.4% YoY) on 39.7% revenue growth, with operating margin at 32.6%. Individuals sold about KRW 71.6bn, and foreigners and quality institutions absorbed it. A buyback result was filed on Nov 13. One caution: ROE is only 7.67%, and the PEAD screen compares year-on-year growth, not consensus surprise. Check next whether Q3 results beat estimates.

**Exicon (092870.KQ)** is a semiconductor test-equipment maker. Operating profit rose 335.4% YoY, margin improved 50.4pp and ROE is 18.1%. The flow contradicts the story, though. Over five days, foreigners and quality institutions net sold KRW 10.0bn, and individuals bought KRW 9.8bn. The next check is whether institutional selling ends and whether the Q3 report confirms the margin jump.

Sungho Electronics (043260.KS) has a thin margin gain of 0.2pp, so the re-rating case is early. Several large-holder reports were filed on Nov 18.

Among single-screener names, Korea Circuit (007810.KS) is worth noting from the PEAD list. Its operating profit rose 1,124.7% YoY from a low base, and its 20-day return is already +28.6%.

These are screened candidates for further research, not recommendations.
