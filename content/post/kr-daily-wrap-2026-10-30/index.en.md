**Bio and pharma bucked the trend.** 삼성바이오로직스 (207940.KS) surged 8.9% and drew institutional accumulation. 한미약품 (128940.KS) added 6.8%. 안랩 (053800.KS) rose 18.0% — the sharpest single-stock move on the session — on what appears to be a standalone catalyst, with foreign investors as net buyers.

**Institutional and foreign flows diverged sharply.** Foreigners sold financials (삼성생명 032830.KS, KB금융 105560.KS) and consumer/auto (에이피알 278470.KS, 현대차 005380.KS, 삼성물산 028260.KS). Institutions countered by accumulating 삼성SDI (006400.KS), 삼성바이오로직스, and 신한지주 (055550.KS) while selling construction.

Derivatives offered mixed signals. Program buy net +4,589억 and ETF creation +21,596억 are positive flow numbers. However, futures basis held negative (–0.33), pointing to a persistent discount on futures relative to spot — a soft risk-appetite signal that qualifies the headline program inflow.
