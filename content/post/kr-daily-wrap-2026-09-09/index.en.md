Korea equities posted a strong five-session run through Sep 9. KOSPI added 472 points (+7.2%) to 7,051.6, and KOSDAQ gained 5.1% to 830.4. The won strengthened nearly 1.5% against the dollar to 1,338 — a signal of favorable external balances and a supportive backdrop for inflows.

Market program selling of −₩322 billion was dominated by non-arbitrage activity (−₩349 billion), consistent with tactical profit-taking rather than structural de-risking.

Despite the index strength, the macro-regime model keeps Korea at Neutral. Breadth has not caught up: only 58.4% of KOSPI constituents trade above their 50-day moving average and just 22.2% above the 200-day. A second confirmation trigger is required before the system upgrades Korea to Bull. Meanwhile, Brent crossing $100 (+5.2% 5-day) adds an inflationary dimension that could complicate the Bank of Korea's rate path — a variable to monitor as commodity prices consolidate.
