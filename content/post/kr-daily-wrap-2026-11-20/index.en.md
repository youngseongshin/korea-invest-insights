## Market Wrap

This was a broad, one-directional selloff. Of 2,585 stocks, 298 rose, 2,089 fell and 198 were unchanged. The average stock lost 2.91%, and the advance/decline ratio proxy was 14.3. The source did not provide turnover for the day.

Derivatives and passive flows leaned bearish. Program trading was net selling of about KRW 1.15 trillion, almost all of it non-arbitrage selling (KRW 1.18 trillion). Futures basis was -2.59. The ETF creation proxy (as of Nov 19) was positive at about KRW 620 billion, so passive money was not leaving the market.

The weakest pockets were cyclical and growth-linked names. Doosan (000150.KS) fell 13.32% and HD Hyundai Construction Equipment (267270.KS) fell 12.53%. Semiconductor equipment and chip-related names also dropped: TSE (131290.KQ) lost 14.59%, HPSP (403870.KQ) 9.03% and DB HiTech (000990.KS) 8.67%. Robotics names fell too, with Robotis (108490.KQ) down 7.16% and Rainbow Robotics (277810.KQ) down 4.00%. Samsung SDI (006400.KS) fell 5.07%, and institutions were top net sellers there.

Foreign net buying went to defensive and energy-linked names rather than growth. The top list was S-Oil (010950.KS), Korea Electric Power (015760.KS), SK Innovation (096770.KS), Samsung Biologics (207940.KS) and Samyang Foods (003230.KS). Foreigners were top sellers of Doosan, NAVER (035420.KS) and Samsung Life (032830.KS). The standout against the tape was Hanmi Pharm (128940.KS), up 7.15% as institutions bought and foreigners sold.

The macro backdrop adds pressure. The VIX is near 29, the US 10-year yield is above 5%, and the dollar index has risen to 103.9. The next scheduled regime check is the morning of Nov 21.

## Today's Quality Re-Rating Candidates

The framework has three layers. First, is the business good (quality)? Second, are foreign and institutional investors buying while retail sells (money flow)? Third, are earnings or estimates improving enough that the market is starting to re-price the stock (re-rating)?

**HMM (011200.KS).** HMM is a container shipping line. It stands out because retail investors sold about KRW 30.0 billion while foreigners and quality institutions absorbed it. Estimates are also rising. It has the longest evidence chain in the table. A November filing on its value-up plan is on record. Check next whether freight rates can hold up if global demand cools.

**Kakao Games (293490.KQ).** Kakao Games makes mobile and PC games. Consensus estimates are moving up, and the stock sits near a breakout level with a 1.4% pivot gap. A corrected filing on an equity acquisition (Nov 16) needs a read-through. Check whether the deal changes the earnings outlook or the balance sheet.

**Amorepacific (090430.KS).** Amorepacific is a cosmetics maker. Foreign and institutional buying of KRW 18.2 billion offset retail selling of KRW 18.0 billion over five days. The stock passes the quiet-accumulation test, meaning foreign ownership is rising within a narrow price range. It also filed a value-up plan on Nov 17. Check next whether Q3 margins support the flow.

**Celltrion (068270.KS) is the cleanest fit for the framework.** It ranks fourth on the Meta Screener but has the most independent evidence (seven families, the highest raw score of 80.8). The Meta order is kept because the quality and cycle families carry less historical weight. Operating profit roughly doubled year on year, and the margin improved 14.2pp. The PEAD screener measures year-on-year growth, not a surprise against consensus. A share buyback result was filed on Nov 13.

**Cautions.** Coway has seen net selling of KRW 8.9 billion by foreigners and quality institutions over five days. Lotte Rental shows KRW 25.5 billion of net selling and two risk-type DART filings, so both are weaker candidates despite their screener hits.

These names are screened candidates, not recommendations. In a Bear regime with only 16.4% breadth, strong relative flows can still fail if the index keeps sliding.
