### Top Three in Focus

**1. Woori Financial Group (316140.KS) — Meta Score 25.9**

Woori is Korea's fourth-largest financial holding company, with commercial banking, credit card, insurance, and capital markets subsidiaries. It tops today's screen with the cleanest risk profile in the financials cluster: short interest at just 2.1% (lowest among the large-cap banks), positive qualitative institutional flow, and an IR event filed with DART as the official catalyst. The RS80 Not-Late signal marks it as a Focus Candidate — pivot gap of +5.1% and box risk of −13.4% indicate price leadership that has not yet reached late-stage overextension. The question for next-level due diligence: is the shareholder return narrative (dividends, buybacks) still ahead of market expectations, or has recent re-rating already captured it?

**2. Amorepacific (090430.KS) — Meta Score 23.1**

Korea's largest beauty company, with flagship brands across skincare and cosmetics and meaningful exposure to China recovery and global premiumization trends. Among the top cluster, Amorepacific carries the widest pivot gap (+9.5%), suggesting the price re-rating is earlier-stage relative to the financials. A buyback completion filing (자기주식처분결과보고서) on September 2 adds a concrete corporate action catalyst. The structural backdrop is favorable: KRW appreciation compresses import costs and strengthens purchasing power for the domestic consumer base. The flag: headline foreign flow is net negative, while qualitative institutional interest is positive — a split signal worth monitoring before drawing a directional conclusion. A foreign-channel sales recovery data point would be the key verification step.

**3. Samsung SDS (018260.KS) — Meta Score 22.5**

Samsung's IT services and logistics arm, increasingly repositioned around AI-driven enterprise solutions, cloud infrastructure, and data center services. The distinguishing signal here is simultaneous positive flow from both foreign (rank #564 by trading volume) and institutional (rank #151) participants — an alignment that is rare in today's screen, where most names show activity from one side but not both. Short interest at 20.4% is elevated and represents a potential overhang, but two DART IR filings in early September indicate active investor engagement. The 20-day flow-and-volume ratio of +11.4% suggests early-stage re-rating momentum is building. The critical next check: whether the AI/cloud enterprise thesis is appearing in sell-side earnings revision data.

### A Structural Note on the Financials Cluster

Four of the top seven ranked names are Korean financials — Woori Financial (316140.KS), Shinhan Financial Group (055550.KS), Hana Financial Group (086790.KS), and iM Financial Group (139130.KS). This is not a coincidence. The RS80 Not-Late screen combined with KRW strength and sustained qualitative institutional buying points to a systematic re-rating of Korean bank valuations. Hana's share cancellation filing is the most explicit capital return signal in the group. With dividend yields above 5% for most of the sector, the re-rating argument has both a flow leg and a valuation leg. The risk: headline foreign flows are net negative for Shinhan and Hana, suggesting some selling into the rally. Investors monitoring this cluster should verify whether consensus earnings revisions are moving in alignment before treating the price action as a confirmed cycle trade rather than a tactical bounce.
