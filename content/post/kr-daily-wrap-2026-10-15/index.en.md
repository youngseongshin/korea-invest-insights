## Market Wrap

Thursday's session was a near-total rout. Of 2,591 stocks tracked in the snapshot, 2,199 fell and only 200 advanced — a market breadth reading of 9.1%. The average stock dropped −3.95%. This is not sector rotation or selective risk-off. It is broad-based, indiscriminate selling.

**The worst-hit cohort.** Small and mid-cap momentum names faced the heaviest institutional exits. The institutional sell list reads like a register of the prior upcycle's winners: 파두 (440110.KQ, −13.75%), 티엘비 (356860.KQ, −15.86%), 엑시콘 (092870.KQ, −12.75%), 로보티즈 (108490.KQ, −12.83%), 두산퓨얼셀 (336260.KS, −13.0%), 브이엠 (089970.KQ, −16.76%), and 인텍플러스 (064290.KQ, −15.16%). Semiconductor equipment, robotics, passive components. Passive component and power cable names — 삼화콘덴서 (001820.KS, −13.4%) and 가온전선 (000500.KS, −13.1%) — were sold simultaneously by both foreigners and institutions, pointing to a coordinated unwind rather than a divergence of views.

**Large-cap consumer and industrial weakness.** Foreigners were net sellers of 삼성물산 (028260.KS, −8.23%), 현대차 (005380.KS, −5.74%), 한화솔루션 (009830.KS, −9.6%), and LG화학 (051910.KS, −5.79%). Retail and department store names fell sharply: 롯데쇼핑 (023530.KS, −8.24%), 신세계 (004170.KS, −7.99%), 현대백화점 (069960.KS, −5.78%). Notably, foreigners were counter-trend net *buyers* of the retail group despite the selling — a flow that may reflect value positioning or index mechanics rather than high-conviction directional views.

**Where institutions rotated.** Against the current, institutions accumulated defensives: 삼성바이오로직스 (207940.KS, +5.50%), KB금융 (105560.KS, −1.57%), 신한지주 (055550.KS, −0.84%), 하나금융지주 (086790.KS, +0.66%), and 우리금융지주 (316140.KS, +0.48%). Large-cap financials and biotech were the primary institutional destinations. 삼양식품 (003230.KS, +0.70%) also held with institutional support — a domestic consumer brand finding a bid in a risk-off tape.

**Derivatives and passive flows.** The ETF net creation proxy printed −18,793억 in net redemptions. Arbitrage program buying (+5,138억) partially offset non-arbitrage selling (−430억). Futures open interest jumped +16,159 contracts. Basis remained mildly positive at +0.60. The setup points to futures-led downside pressure with selective institutional accumulation in large-cap defensives and healthcare — not a panic liquidation, but not a buyers' market either.
