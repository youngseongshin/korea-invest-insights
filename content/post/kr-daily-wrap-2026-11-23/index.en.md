## Market Wrap

This was a broad risk-off session. Of 2,593 stocks, 339 rose, 2,051 fell and 203 were flat. The average stock lost 2.44%, and the advance-decline proxy sat at 16.5. The source snapshot has no trading-value data, so turnover is not covered.

The damage was uneven. IT services and platforms fell hardest: NHN (181710.KS) dropped 18.7%, Samsung SDS (018260.KS) 15.4% and LG CNS (064400.KS) 13.9%. NAVER (035420.KS) fell 7.2% and Kakao (035720.KS) 6.6%. Construction and energy-linked names were also weak. GS Engineering & Construction (006360.KS) lost 12.6%, and GS Holdings (078930.KS) lost 8.1%. KB Financial (105560.KS) fell 5.7%.

Pockets of strength were few. Amorepacific's rival APR (278470.KQ) rose 9.1%. Robotics-related names such as Robotis (108490.KQ) gained 5.4%, and Doosan (000150.KS) rose 2.7%. Domestic institutions appear among the top net buyers in those names.

Flow signals were mixed. Foreigners were among the top net sellers of NAVER, KB Financial and Samsung Biologics (207940.KS). They also appeared on the buy list for several fallers, including GS and LG Innotek (011070.KS), so some of them were buying the dip. Institutions sold NAVER, Samsung SDS and NHN heavily. Net amounts are not disclosed in the source.

On derivatives, program trading was a net +204.5 billion won, but futures basis was -4.18. That points to weak futures sentiment even though the cash market was supported. ETF net creations were +796.8 billion won as of Nov 20.

## Today's Quality Re-Rating Candidates

The framework looks for good businesses where money is entering and the market is starting to re-price the story. It uses three layers:

- **Quality:** profitable companies with strong returns on equity.
- **Money flow:** foreign and institutional buying.
- **Re-rating trigger:** margin expansion, earnings upgrades and official catalysts.

No stock appeared in two or more of the core screeners on a simple overlap count. **Kakao Games (293490.KQ)** is a game publisher. It matches the consensus-upgrade and relative-strength screens. The stock sits about 2% from its pivot point, with a box risk of -15.2%. DART shows an IR event on Nov 5, a Q3 report on Nov 13 and an amended equity-acquisition filing on Nov 16. The next check is what that acquisition adds to earnings.

**Exicon (092870.KQ)** has the strongest fundamentals of the group: operating profit up 335% year on year and ROE of 18.1%. It passes five independent evidence families. The flow layer is the weak point. Foreigners and quality institutions sold a net 6.9 billion won over five days, and retail absorbed 8.0 billion won. The next check is whether that selling turns into buying.

**SK hynix (000660.KS)** is the memory chipmaker. Earnings and consensus point up, with a consensus z-score of +0.51. Flow is the open question. Foreigners and quality institutions sold a net 1.38 trillion won over five days, while retail also sold 818 billion won. The next check is whether the selling pressure fades or the price keeps falling with it.

In the PEAD screen, Kosses (089890.KQ) was the only name flagged. Its operating profit rose 196% year on year and revenue 50%. This screen uses year-on-year growth, not an earnings surprise against consensus.

## What to Watch

These are screened candidates, not recommendations. In a Bear regime with VIX near 30, a single-day screen can flag a stock that keeps falling. The next regime check is scheduled for Nov 24 at 08:20 KST.
