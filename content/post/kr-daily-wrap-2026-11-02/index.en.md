PEAD Tier A data shows operating profit up 334% YoY with margin expansion of 11.1pp. Over the past five sessions, foreign buyers and qualified institutions absorbed ₩20.7bn of retail supply. The critical verification step: determine whether the 334% profit growth reflects a one-time base effect or a structural step-up in test socket capacity and pricing power. If structural, the re-rating thesis has legs.

**Koses (089890.KQ)** makes semiconductor cleaning and deposition equipment. Four screeners hit, including a Consensus Up Revision (z-score +0.53) — meaning analysts are marking estimates higher. The headline number is a 29.2pp operating margin expansion, an unusually large move that demands explanation before the re-rating thesis is accepted. Two supply contract amendment filings appeared in DART on Oct 27 and Nov 2. Smart money flows are constructive (5-day F+QI net +₩1.64bn against retail −₩12.37bn), but the margin story dominates. Next step: match the contract amendments to the revenue and margin line to determine whether the expansion is mix-shift, volume leverage, or a one-quarter anomaly.

**Harim Holdings (003380.KS)** is the listed holding company for Korea's largest poultry and food distribution group, with significant logistics assets. Three screeners fired: Cycle Rerating (margin +0.5pp), RS80 Not-Late Leadership (pivot gap +2.7%, box risk −13.5% — price leadership without overextension), and Smart Money Quality (5-day F+QI +₩3.15bn, retail −₩2.92bn). Two material DART events in the past two weeks: a subsidiary spin-off decision on Oct 26 and a large supply contract on Oct 22. The spin-off is the structural catalyst to examine — if it isolates logistics assets into a separately valued entity, the holding company discount could narrow. Verify spin-off terms and target listing timeline before treating the catalyst as confirmed.
