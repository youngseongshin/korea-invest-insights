## Market Wrap

The Korea stock market bounced hard this week, but participation is still narrow. KOSPI is up 5.4% and KOSDAQ 9.5% over five days. Only 21.0% of the 2,692 stocks screened trade above their 50-day average. For the 200-day average the share is 15.6%. Both KOSPI and KOSDAQ remain below their 200-day averages.

Today was selective. Of 2,592 stocks, 1,196 rose, 1,130 fell and 266 were flat. The advance/decline ratio was 105.8, and the average stock gained 0.20%.

Financials led. KB Financial (105560.KS) gained 4.74%. Hana Financial (086790.KS) rose 3.40%. BNK Financial (138930.KS) climbed 6.48%. Brokers also moved up. Samsung Securities (016360.KS) added 3.50% and NH Investment & Securities (005940.KS) 3.39%.

Utilities and internet names lagged. KEPCO (015760.KS) dropped 9.79%. NAVER (035420.KS) fell 5.12%. Doosan (000150.KS) lost 8.55%.

Flow data pointed in two directions. Foreign investors were top net buyers of Samsung SDI (006400.KS), KB Financial, Hyundai Motor (005380.KS) and Hana Financial. They were top net sellers of KEPCO, NAVER and Samsung Securities. Institutions bought brokers and financials, including Samsung Securities, Korea Investment Holdings (071050.KS) and NH Investment & Securities. They sold NAVER, KEPCO and Doosan. The source gives rankings only, with no amounts.

The derivatives picture is mixed. Program trading showed net buying of about 339 billion won on Nov 12. Futures basis was -6.07, which means futures traded below spot. ETF net creation was about 1.55 trillion won on Nov 11. These figures are one to two sessions old.

## Today's Quality Re-Rating Candidates

The framework has three layers. **Quality** asks whether the business earns good returns. **Money flow** asks whether foreign and institutional buyers are absorbing retail selling. **Re-rating** asks whether earnings or the cycle are improving enough for the market to reprice the stock.

The KR Meta Screener ranks names by family score. Historically, the financial-factor families (Quality, Smart Money Quality, Cycle, Smart Money Earnings) added little in forward-return tests. Estimate-upgrade signals added the most. The meta order is therefore not the same as a simple overlap count.
