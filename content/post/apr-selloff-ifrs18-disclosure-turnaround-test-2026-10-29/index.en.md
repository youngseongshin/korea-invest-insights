APR closed at **₩289,000** on October 28. That is **38% below its August 31 close** of ₩464,500. The worst day was October 13, when the stock fell **19.6%** while the KOSPI fell 5.5%. It was APR's largest one-day fall since its February 2024 listing.

The trigger was not a public filing. On October 12, APR reportedly held a **Q3 preview call** with some analysts. Several brokers cut their Q3 profit estimates the next day. [Digital Post, Oct 21](https://www.ilovepc.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=61049). On October 20, the Korea Exchange gave notice that it may designate APR an **"unfaithful disclosure" company** for failing to publicly disclose its preliminary Q3 operating results under the fair-disclosure rules.

The market read the episode as a margin break: an exporter hit by a stronger won and by expensive air freight to Europe. **Our judgment is different. Most of the margin scare comes from the won, through translation and through one accounting line that APR now reports inside operating profit. Much of the growth scare comes from a calendar shift in Amazon's Prime Day. The October 30 results can settle the margin question. They cannot settle the question that sets the multiple: whether US sales still grow in dollars.**

This article is dated October 29, 2026, the day before APR's 10 a.m. KST Q3 earnings call. Prices are KRX regular-session closes. Q3 figures below are broker estimates or our calculations, not company results.

## Five things to take away

- **APR is one of very few Korean listed companies already reporting under IFRS 18.** The new standard puts foreign-exchange gains and losses on receivables and payables inside operating profit. In the first half that line added ₩11.2 billion. In Q3, the won rose 12.4% between quarter-ends, so the same line likely turned into a loss.
- **On a like-for-like basis, the margin looks stable.** Excluding that FX line, Q2's operating margin was 24.2%. Korea Investment & Securities estimates Q3 at 24.3–24.6% on the old basis.
- **The flat US quarter is largely a calendar effect.** Amazon moved Prime Day 2026 to June 23–26, inside Q2. Last year it fell in Q3. The same broker estimates US sales flat from Q2 at constant currency.
- **Europe "sold too well," and the fix is logistics, not demand.** APR's Q2 air freight bill was about ₩30 billion, equal to roughly a fifth of its European revenue, though not all of it was for Europe. The company called it largely temporary. Shifting to sea freight and local warehouses is an execution task.
- **The real test is Q4 in dollars, then Q1 reorders.** Consensus expects Q4 revenue up 19% in won, about 26% in dollars at October exchange rates. New Costco shelves, and CVS by year-end, will help Q4 sell-in. Whether US shoppers keep buying shows up in Q1 2027.

October 30 is the first time all investors see the same numbers.

## IFRS 18 turned the won into an operating cost

IFRS 18 becomes mandatory in Korea for fiscal years starting in 2027. APR adopted it early, from January 1, 2026, and restated its comparison periods. [APR half-year report](https://dart.fss.or.kr/dsaf001/main.do?rcpNo=20260814002503). Under the new standard, operating profit is the residual category. FX differences on trade receivables and payables move into it. Most Korean companies still report those gains and losses below operating profit.

APR earns about 92% of its revenue abroad and invoices much of it in dollars. When the won weakens, its dollar receivables gain value and operating profit rises. When the won strengthens, the opposite happens. The half-year report shows the line clearly:

| Quarter | Quarter-end USD/KRW change | Net FX gain (loss) in operating profit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Q1 2025 | +0.3% | +₩0.5bn |
| Q2 2025 | -8.4% | -₩2.8bn |
| Q1 2026 | +4.5% | +₩6.7bn |
| Q2 2026 | +3.0% | +₩4.5bn |
| Q3 2026 | -12.4% | Not yet reported |

FX gains and losses are the sum of the "operating" FX items in note 22 of the half-year report; Q1 is the half-year total minus Q2. Quarter-end rates are Yahoo Finance USD/KRW closes. A positive rate change means a weaker won.

In 2026, the line moved by about **₩1.5 billion for each 1% change** in the quarter-end exchange rate. In Q2 2025, on smaller balances, the ratio was about ₩0.3 billion. Applied to Q3's 12.4% move, the 2026 ratio suggests an operating FX loss of ₩15–20 billion. The gap between Korea Investment & Securities' old-basis estimate below and the reported-basis consensus implies about ₩10–12 billion. Either range is a large part of the ₩16 billion cut in consensus operating profit, from ₩197 billion to ₩181 billion. These are estimates; APR has not reported the figure.

Korea Investment & Securities makes the same point from the other direction. It estimates Q3 operating margin on the old basis at **24.3–24.6%**, compared with a pre-cut market expectation of 25.2%. It also estimates that Q3 margin would have been 0.7–1.0 percentage points higher if exchange rates had stayed at Q2 levels, and that European air freight raised the cost of sales ratio by about 0.5 points. [S-Journal, Oct 23](https://www.s-journal.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=44755). For comparison, Q2's reported margin of 24.8% falls to **24.2%** once the ₩4.5 billion FX gain is removed.

![Net FX gains and losses inside APR's operating profit by quarter, with the quarter-end change in USD/KRW. Q1 2025 +₩0.5bn, Q2 2025 -₩2.8bn, Q1 2026 +₩6.7bn, Q2 2026 +₩4.5bn. For Q3 2026, the 12.4% quarter-end move implies a loss of roughly ₩10–20bn, an estimate range.](/post-assets/apr-selloff-ifrs18-disclosure-turnaround-test-2026-10-29/ifrs18-fx-line.en.png)

**Two different exchange rates matter, and investors tend to merge them.** The IFRS 18 line depends on the quarter-end rate. Revenue translation depends on the quarterly average. In Q3, both hurt: the average rate fell 5.6% and the quarter-end rate fell 12.4%. In Q4 so far, the average is 5.7% below Q3's, but the rate has moved only about 1% since September 30. If rates hold, Q4 keeps the translation headwind but loses most of the IFRS 18 hit. Reported margin would then recover mechanically from Q3, with no change in the business.

## The flat US quarter is mostly a calendar

The second worry is growth. Consensus Q3 revenue is ₩786.1 billion, only 2.4% above Q2. Last year, Q3 revenue rose 17.8% from Q2. For a stock valued on growth, that slowdown matters more than any margin point.

Most of it has a mundane explanation. Amazon held Prime Day 2026 on **June 23–26**, so APR's biggest US online event fell in Q2. In 2025, Prime Day fell in July, inside Q3. [Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-prime-day-june-2026). Korea Investment & Securities estimates US sales fell 2.6% from Q2 in won, and were roughly flat at constant exchange rates. In dollars, flat sales in a quarter without Prime Day is not evidence of saturation.

The October data point the same way. At Amazon's Prime Big Deal Days on October 6–7, APR set new daily sales records in both the US and Europe, and US sales were reported up 85% from the same event a year earlier. Medicube also started selling in about 400 US Costco stores this month. [Bizwatch, Oct 15](https://news.bizwatch.co.kr/article/consumer/2026/10/15/0025), [S-Journal, Oct 23](https://www.s-journal.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=44755), [Korea Times, Oct 19](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/companies/20261019/apr-expands-us-retail-presence-as-medicube-hits-costco-shelves).

## Europe sold too well, and paid for it in air freight

APR moved from distributors to direct sales on Amazon in the UK, France, Germany, Italy and Spain in late 2025. That means APR now carries the shipping and inventory itself. European revenue reached ₩145.1 billion in Q2, 19% of the total, and Kyobo Securities estimates ₩171.7 billion in Q3.

Demand ran ahead of stock. APR ran short of inventory in Europe in Q1 and turned to air freight. Its Q2 air freight bill was about **₩30 billion**, nearly ten times a year earlier. Disruption to sea routes and flight paths around the Middle East made it worse. The company says urgent volumes still go by air, and it is preparing to ship ahead by sea and serve orders from local warehouses. In August it called the Q2 air bill largely temporary, citing the earlier Prime Day and the Iran war's disruption to sea routes. Korea Investment & Securities nonetheless estimates that air use rose again in Q3. [Bizwatch, Oct 15](https://news.bizwatch.co.kr/article/consumer/2026/10/15/0025), [Etoday, Aug 5](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2611246).

₩30 billion is roughly a fifth of Q2 European revenue, though not all of it may relate to Europe. Even so, the scale shows the lever. Sea freight costs a fraction of air. Replacing half of the air volume would be worth well over one percentage point of group margin. The risk is timing: air freight falls only after inventory is positioned ahead of demand.

## The test that October 30 cannot pass for APR

The margin questions are answerable this week. The growth question is not.

Consensus expects **Q4 revenue of ₩938.1 billion, up 19% from Q3** in won. At October's average exchange rate, that is about **26% in dollars**. That looks demanding, but Q4 is APR's strongest quarter: last year, Q4 revenue rose 42% from Q3. The company's own target of about ₩3 trillion of 2026 revenue needs only about ₩853 billion in Q4, if Q3 lands at consensus. In August, APR raised that target from ₩2.1 trillion and kept its operating margin guidance of 24–26%. [Etoday, Aug 5](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2611246).

Here is the skeptical point. Q4 will include the first stocking orders from new US retail partners: Costco from October, and CVS, planned by year-end. Walmart shelves arrived in June. [Korea Times, Oct 19](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/companies/20261019/apr-expands-us-retail-presence-as-medicube-hits-costco-shelves). Initial sell-in flatters revenue. The honest test of US demand is whether those retailers reorder in **Q1 2027**, and whether Amazon sales keep growing in dollars between promotional events. Consensus also expects 2027 revenue of ₩4.27 trillion, 38% above 2026. That number assumes the US engine keeps running.

## Valuation and flows

At ₩289,000, APR trades at about **19.0 times 2026 and 13.5 times 2027 consensus EPS** (Naver/WiseReport, October 28). The 2027 multiple is low for a company still growing revenue more than 30%, if that growth holds. It is not low if US growth is plateauing; then 2027 earnings are too high.

Foreign investors bought APR shares on each of the four trading days from October 23 to 28, while domestic institutions sold. Foreign ownership rose from 39.0% to 39.6%. [Naver Finance](https://m.stock.naver.com/domestic/stock/278470/total). That is a modest signal, not proof that selling is over.

## Our judgment, and what would change it

**We think the turnaround case is stronger than the price suggests on margin, and still unproven on growth.** The two things that broke the stock, the margin scare and the disclosure episode, are the two things October 30 can address. The thing that sets the multiple, US growth in dollars, needs Q4 and Q1 data.

Three variables decide it. **US dollar sales growth** dominates, because it sets whether 2027 earnings are real. **The won** comes second; it moves reported profit up and down without changing the business. **Europe logistics** comes third; it is a fixable cost.

| Evidence on October 30 or after | What it would mean |
|---|---|
| APR shows Q3 operating profit on the old basis, with margin at 24% or above | The "margin break" was an FX classification effect; the business margin held |
| Management reiterates about ₩3 trillion of 2026 revenue and the 24–26% margin range, and describes October US sales | Q4 is on track; the flat Q3 was mainly the Prime Day calendar |
| Old-basis margin below about 23% | Logistics or marketing costs are structural, not a one-quarter problem |
| Q4 US sales flat in dollars, or weak Q1 2027 reorders from new retailers | US growth is plateauing; the 2027 consensus is too high |
| Exchange penalty of 10 points or more | A listing-eligibility review becomes possible; the governance discount widens |

The next checks are close. APR's objection deadline to the exchange notice is **October 29**. Its Q3 earnings call is **October 30 at 10 a.m. KST**, with English interpretation. The exchange's final decision on the disclosure designation follows.
