Samsung Electronics closed at <strong>₩188,700</strong> on November 6. That is 2.6 to 2.9 times 12-month forward earnings and 1.17 times book value. On the same day, the average target price of the 21 brokers tracked by FnGuide was <strong>₩472,381</strong>, and 20 of the 21 rated the stock a buy. The average target is 2.5 times the share price.

To explain that gap, we built two separate valuation models of Samsung. The first is a scenario grid that splits the timing and depth of the memory price turn into 96 cases. The second is a cash-flow model that projects cash flows and returns on capital out to 2040. They came out at <strong>₩232,600</strong> and <strong>₩291,300</strong> per share, ₩59,000 apart.

When we took the gap apart one assumption at a time, the picture became simple. Both models and the consensus put 2027 operating profit near ₩600 trillion. They split on 2028 prices, on how long excess profits last after 2029, and on what happens to the cash that piles up. Load the bear assumptions into the cash-flow model at the same time and it lands at <strong>₩195,300</strong>, almost exactly today's price.

<strong>Our view: today's price only makes sense if a 2028 profit decline, the end of excess profits in 2030 and a low return on retained cash all come true at once. We read Samsung as conditionally undervalued. Our working range is ₩233,000 to ₩291,000, with a center near ₩260,000, 38% above the close. The condition is the shareholder-return policy from the board meeting in late January 2027. If returns stay undecided and capex rises 30% or more, we move our view toward ₩196,000.</strong>

- 2027 operating profit converges at ₩600 trillion to ₩630 trillion. Fourth-quarter exit prices and long-term contracts tie the numbers down. Eight brokers cluster between ₩604 trillion and ₩644 trillion.
- 2028 splits from ₩532 trillion (−11%) to ₩684 trillion (+9%). Among brokers the spread runs from ₩337 trillion to ₩939 trillion. Nobody has yet observed when prices turn.
- 67% to 72% of the value comes from 2029 onward. So the gap between the models comes more from the life of excess profits and the treatment of retained cash than from 2027 to 2028 profit.
- The ₩59,000 gap is 2028 profit, the life of excess profits, a permanent excess return and retained cash, each worth ₩30,000 to ₩40,000, pulling in opposite directions.
- Retained cash is the first variable we will observe. The board meeting in late January 2027 gives the first answer.

Before comparing numbers, align the definitions. Our two models give a present value as of today. Broker targets are 12-month prices, so the ₩472,381 average discounts to about ₩429,000 over one year.

So we restated the targets of five brokers (Hana, IBK, KB, Shinhan and iM) to the same date, the same dividend rights and a 12% discount rate. Their average present value is ₩424,100, from ₩331,300 at IBK to ₩554,100 at KB. FnGuide targets run from ₩270,000 at BNK (hold) to ₩690,000 at Yuanta.

The horizontal bars are each model's own stated range, not confidence intervals. The grid bar is the 10th to 90th percentile of its 96 cases (₩183,800 to ₩283,100). The cash-flow bar runs from a downside of ₩107,100, with the bad assumptions stacked, to an upside of ₩525,400.

The hollow markers are numbers we threw away. ₩164,400 came from a first model built on one broker's detailed estimate table, which we replaced with a skeleton built from company filings and industry data. ₩201,400 came from an earlier grid that drew more downside paths than upside paths; we replaced it when we put up and down cases on the same axes. Every model still standing has its center above today's price.

## Everyone uses the same 2027 profit

The only reported results are 2025 operating profit of ₩43.7 trillion and ₩254.1 trillion for the first nine months of 2026 (₩57.2 trillion, ₩89.5 trillion and ₩107.4 trillion by quarter). Everything after that is an estimate.

All three put 2026 at ₩383 trillion to ₩388 trillion, adding ₩129 trillion to ₩134 trillion for the fourth quarter. The company's shipment guidance and TrendForce's fourth-quarter contract-price outlook (+10% to 15%) tie that number down.

2027 converges too. The scenario grid has ₩600.2 trillion, the cash-flow model ₩629.5 trillion and the FnGuide consensus ₩606.6 trillion, all within ±5% of one another. Eight brokers cluster between ₩604 trillion and ₩644 trillion. At Hana, 86% of the 2027 estimate is the fourth-quarter exit price run for a full year. Most of the 2027 estimates are tied to this year's closing prices.

The quality of that profit overlaps as well. The share of operating profit left as profit for shareholders is 76% to 77% (76.9% in the grid, a 76.3% broker median). Several sources put 2027 HBM revenue at ₩115 trillion to ₩117 trillion.

The exception is Kiwoom. It alone has profit falling, to ₩380 trillion in 2027 and ₩337 trillion in 2028. We could not find the price table behind it in public material.

## From 2028 the numbers split, because nobody knows when prices turn

2028 operating profit is ₩532.3 trillion in the grid (−11.3%), ₩684.0 trillion in the cash-flow model (+8.7%) and ₩668.8 trillion in the consensus (+10.2%). Among brokers it runs from ₩337.4 trillion at Kiwoom (−11%) to ₩939.3 trillion at IBK (+32%).

The consensus +10% is an agreement in appearance only. It averages a range from −11% to +32%. The cash-flow model's +9% sits on that average, but it was built a different way.

The split comes from one fact: no one has yet observed when memory prices turn. The grid uses the length of past upcycles as its base rate. The last four lasted 9, 3, 3 and 5 quarters. This one reaches its sixth quarter in the fourth quarter of 2026, longer than three of the four. So the grid puts 85% of its weight on prices turning by the first quarter of 2028. That weight is a subjective judgment, not a calibrated probability.

The cash-flow model and the brokers extend the company's call for "a deeper shortage in 2027" and its long-term contracts into 2028. The cash-flow model's ₩684 trillion assumes conventional DRAM prices −10%, NAND −20% and flat HBM prices, with DRAM shipments +18%, HBM +35%, NAND +20% and cost per bit −5%. That keeps the conventional memory operating margin almost unchanged, from 87.9% to 87.2%. In the grid's distribution, ₩684 trillion sits in roughly the top quarter.

The grid's expected quarterly path peaks at ₩154.7 trillion in the second quarter of 2027 and bottoms at ₩128.9 trillion in the second quarter of 2028. If prices turn early, in the first quarter of 2027, profit falls to ₩94.8 trillion by the fourth quarter of 2027. If prices do not turn through 2028, it climbs to ₩222.3 trillion by the fourth quarter of 2028.

Broker quarterly tables do not answer the question either. Hana, DS and Daishin go from +4% to 16% in the first quarter of 2027 to −1% to +1% by the fourth quarter. None of them shows a price fall the size of past downturns.

## But value is decided from 2029 on

In the grid, 72% of the value comes from the present value of book equity at the end of 2029. In the cash-flow model, 67% of operating value comes from 2029 onward. The fight over 2027 and 2028 profit is a fight over about a third of the value.

![How the two models treat operating profit after 2028, ₩ trillion. The grid goes from 641 in 2029 straight to a steady state from 2030 (net profit ₩167 trillion, about 220 in operating-profit terms). The cash-flow model steps down over five years, 593 in 2029, 501 in 2030, 407 in 2031, 310 in 2032 and 211 in 2033, then holds at 183 in 2036 and 202 in 2040. The area between the lines and the permanent excess return are the gap between the models.](/post-assets/samsung-188700-prices-three-bear-cases-at-once-2026-11-08/long-run.en.svg)

The grid drops from ₩641 trillion in 2029 to a steady state in 2030 in one step. Net profit falls 66%, from ₩492 trillion to ₩167 trillion. In the steady state, return on equity (ROE) of 11% equals the 11% discount rate, so excess profit is zero.

The cash-flow model fades excess profit on conventional memory over five years and on high-value products such as HBM over eight. After that, it lets capital used in production earn 18% forever, against a 12% required return. From 2036 on, the two models show similar profit levels. The gap is the area between them in 2030 to 2033 and that permanent excess return.

Samsung's own record answers part of the question. Its ROE from 2017 to 2025 averaged 12.7% with a median of 10.8%. The longest run of years above 17% is two. Micron's longest is three.

![Samsung Electronics ROE attributable to owners, DART consolidated, percent. 2017 21.0, 2018 19.6, 2019 8.7, 2020 10.0, 2021 13.9, 2022 17.1, 2023 4.1, 2024 9.0, 2025 10.8. Average 12.7%, median 10.8%. Lines mark the terminal ROE today's price requires (8.1%), the grid's terminal ROE (11%) and the lowest terminal ROE the average target requires (19.5%).](/post-assets/samsung-188700-prices-three-bear-cases-at-once-2026-11-08/roe-history.en.svg)

The cash-flow model's 18% is a return on operating capital (ROIC) that excludes non-operating cash, so it does not compare directly with ROE. Even so, a permanent 6-point excess return is more optimistic than Samsung's nine-year record. Micron's average ROE depends on the sample: 14.0% for fiscal 2011 to 2025, 18.7% with fiscal 2026 included.

What happens to the cash that piles up is another large assumption. At a 33% payout ratio, equity attributable to owners grows from ₩608 trillion in 2026 to ₩1,521 trillion in 2029, 2.5 times. The grid lets that money earn 11% on the books. The cash-flow model returns half at once and half after five years, earning 2.5% in the meantime. Most brokers do not state this assumption.

Both models point at the same variables with similar weight. Variables after 2029 (the life of excess profits, retained cash and the discount rate) are larger than 2027 to 2028 profit.

Reweighting the timing and depth of the price turn 16 different ways moves the grid's value by −₩16,300 to +₩18,800. Extending the life of excess profits by just three years moves it by ₩31,200.

## The ₩59,000 gap is levers cancelling each other out

We kept the cash-flow model's code as it is and switched its assumptions, one at a time, to the grid's settings.

![Bridge from the cash-flow model's ₩291,300 to the scenario grid's ₩232,600, ₩ thousand. 2028 operating profit 684 to 532 trillion −31.4; immediate normalization in 2030 −28.5; permanent ROIC 18% to 12% −36.2, which brings the running total to 195.3, almost today's 188.7. Retained cash returned at once +38.6; no discount on investment assets +2.9; required return and steady-state ROIC both 11% +4.1; 6.519 billion shares +4.8; structural residual −13.1; scenario grid 232.6.](/post-assets/samsung-188700-prices-three-bear-cases-at-once-2026-11-08/bridge.en.svg)

| Step | Assumption changed | Change (₩ thousand) | Running total (₩ thousand) |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Start | Cash-flow model as built | | 291.3 |
| 1 | 2028 operating profit ₩684 trillion to ₩532 trillion | −31.4 | 259.9 |
| 2 | Excess profit kept through 2029, zero from 2030 | −28.5 | 231.4 |
| 3 | Steady-state ROIC 18% to 12% (no permanent excess return) | −36.2 | 195.3 |
| 4 | Retained cash: half after five years to all at once | +38.6 | 233.8 |
| 5 | Remove the 20% discount on investment assets | +2.9 | 236.8 |
| 6 | Required return and steady-state ROIC 12% to 11% | +4.1 | 240.8 |
| 7 | Shares 6.649 billion to 6.519 billion (buybacks) | +4.8 | 245.6 |
| 8 | Structural differences (2027 and 2029 profit, treatment of cash and investments) | −13.1 | 232.6 |

After step three the running total is ₩195,300, almost the share price. At that point retained cash still follows the cash-flow model's own assumption: half comes back five years late. Today's price is therefore the price at which an 11% profit decline in 2028, excess profit vanishing in 2030, no permanent excess return and late return of retained cash all hold together.

The grid finds the same spot. If half of retained capital sits as cash earning 3%, it gives ₩195,900. A terminal ROE of 8.1% also reproduces today's price. That is below Samsung's nine-year median of 10.8%.

The order of the steps changes the size of each bar. Switch the valuation assumptions first and immediate normalization is −₩38,600, the permanent excess return −₩36,200, cash +₩43,200 and 2028 profit −₩26,600. The shape is the same: each lever is ₩30,000 to ₩40,000, and they point in opposite directions.

Most broker targets are book value per share times a price-to-book multiple: 4.6 times at KB, 4.3 at IBK, 3.2 at Shinhan and 2.3 at iM. That puts a normal multiple on peak profit. Hana raised its profit estimate by 20% and lowered its multiple to keep a ₩480,000 target.

Since the end of June the average target has stayed between ₩465,000 and ₩494,000, while the stock fell 43.5%, from ₩334,000 to ₩188,700. The record runs the other way too. The average target of ₩139,385 on December 30, 2025, was far below the ₩334,000 the stock reached six months later. Targets tend to lag in upcycles and stay put after the peak.

The 12-month horizon has not expired, so we do not treat this as proof the targets are wrong. On the same profit path, changing only the valuation assumptions gives ₩161,500 to ₩485,400, which reaches both our numbers and the targets. We do not rule on who is wrong. We say where today's price sits, below the center of all three, and which variables make the gap.

## Our view: conditionally undervalued, but we do not center on ₩291,300

If each model gives up one assumption, they meet. Give the grid a three-year fade in excess profits and it reaches ₩263,800. Give the cash-flow model the grid's 2028 profit and it reaches ₩259,900. So our working range is ₩233,000 to ₩291,000 with a center near ₩260,000. That center approximates two points; it is not a probability-weighted expected value.

There are reasons not to center on the cash-flow model's ₩291,300. Its 2028 profit of ₩684 trillion sits in roughly the top quarter of the grid's distribution and keeps an 87% conventional memory margin through 2028. A permanent 6-point excess return does not fit Samsung's nine-year median ROE of 10.8%. Its 2027 starting point is the broker median, so it is not independent evidence. Its outside reviewer also approved it on the condition that the model as a whole is not called conservative.

There are reasons not to keep the grid's ₩232,600 either. The cliff from ₩492 trillion of net profit in 2029 to ₩167 trillion in 2030 is an input, not a result. The grid started from the possibility of a profit decline, and it says so: it leans toward downside stress.

The loss paths are real. In the cash-flow model, if conventional memory excess profit is zero from 2029, value is ₩236,100; if conventional memory loses money in 2030 and 2031, it is ₩208,300. With the bad assumptions stacked, it is ₩107,100. In the grid, 12.5% of cases sit below today's price, and in the cases where prices turn in the first quarter of 2027, value falls to ₩157,000 to ₩184,000. A center above the price does not mean the risk of loss is small.

We also have a warning sign of our own. Over November 7 and 8, our own value estimate rose from ₩201,400 to ₩218,000, ₩225,000, ₩232,600 and then ₩291,300, while the stock was falling. The reasons were a corrected HBM input and a more symmetric scenario design. Still, a number moving against the price looks like the broker targets that never followed the stock down. So we will recalculate only after first-hand observations come in.

## The board meeting in late January gives the first verdict

Retained cash is the first variable we will observe. The two models bet −₩36,700 (grid) and −₩38,200 to +₩59,500 (cash-flow model) on this one lever.

These lines mark where we will look at the view again. They are not automatic trading rules.
