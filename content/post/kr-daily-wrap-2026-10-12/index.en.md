Foreign buying elsewhere was selective and anti-trend: Samsung C&T (028260.KS) and Samyang Foods (003230.KS) received inflows while posting modest declines or gains, suggesting positioning rather than momentum. The overall read is rotational accumulation, not broad conviction.

**Session character:** Risk-off, systematic. Sector strength: shipping, defensive financials. Sector weakness: LG conglomerate, construction, select tech. Program selling of −₩1.07T can persist mechanically beyond a single session.
