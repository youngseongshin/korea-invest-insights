## Market Wrap

The KOSPI session was a broad, sharp rebound inside a downtrend. Of 2,599 stocks, 1,978 rose and 403 fell, and the average stock gained 2.87%. Even so, the KOSPI is still about 1.2% below its Nov 18 level. Only a small share of stocks sit above their medium-term averages, so this looks like a bounce, not a confirmed trend change. The regime model logs it as "rebound attempt, Day 1."

Large caps with sharp moves led the day. Autos, batteries and robotics stood out: Samsung SDI (006400.KS) rose 13.6%, Hyundai Motor (005380.KS) 11.1%, LG Electronics (066570.KS) 15.9%, and Hyundai Mobis (012330.KS) 14.5%. Robotics names ran hardest. Robotis (108490.KQ) gained 19.5% and Rainbow Robotics (277810.KQ) 17.4%. Hyundai AutoEver (307950.KS) jumped 29.9%.

Flows were mixed, and the stocks that rose the most are where foreigners and institutions disagreed.
- Foreigners and institutions both appeared among the top net buyers of Samsung SDI, Hyundai Motor, Robotis and Rainbow Robotics.
- Foreigners were top net sellers of Doosan (000150.KS), Hyundai Mobis and LG Energy Solution (373220.KS), while institutions were top net buyers of all three.
- Institutions were top net sellers of Samsung Biologics (207940.KS), SK Innovation (096770.KS) and Korea Electric Power (015760.KS).

Net-buy amounts were redacted in the source, so the size of each flow is unconfirmed.

Derivatives data was less supportive. Program trading was net positive at about 376.5 billion won, but futures basis was -0.58, and ETF net creation was about -2.0 trillion won as of Nov 23. The source rules need a basis of at least 1.0 and breadth above 35% to call program buying a Bull signal. Neither condition was met.

External backdrop: the VIX near 29 is the main risk. Falling Brent and easing US yields are the offsets.

**HMM (011200.KS)** is a container shipper. It ranks first because three independent signals line up: analysts are raising numbers, foreign holdings are rising in a narrow price range, and price leadership is early rather than stretched. Individuals sold about 25.6 billion won, and foreigners plus quality institutions absorbed it. Today's DART filings are shareholder-meeting documents, so there is no earnings catalyst yet. Next check: whether estimate revisions continue.

**Exicon (092870.KQ)** has the strongest numbers. Operating profit more than tripled year on year, margin widened 50.4 points, and revenue rose about 306%. It also appears in the PEAD screen. The caution is flow: foreigners and quality institutions were net sellers of about 5.6 billion won over five days, and individuals absorbed it. Next check: whether institutional selling reverses.

**Kakao Games (293490.KQ)** is a thinner case. It shows rising estimates and early leadership, plus two official catalyst filings. The most recent filings, including an amended acquisition decision on Nov 16, are neutral. Next check: details of that acquisition and the next estimate revisions.

These are screened candidates for further research, not trade recommendations.
