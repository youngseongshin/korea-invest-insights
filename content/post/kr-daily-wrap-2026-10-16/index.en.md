### Deep Dive: Top 3 Names

**1. Celltrion (068270.KS) — Score 64.6, 6 evidence families**

Celltrion is Korea's leading biosimilar manufacturer, with commercial products covering oncology, autoimmune, and infectious disease indications. It is the only name today reaching six independent evidence families. The key signal is a margin expansion of +14.2 percentage points in the most recent reporting period, combined with ₩144.2bn in net foreign-plus-institutional buying over the past five days — while retail investors sold ₩226bn into that demand. When smart money absorbs aggressive retail distribution at this scale, it warrants scrutiny. Three recent DART filings strengthen the case: a Phase 3 IND approval for CT-P13 SC (Zymfentra, subcutaneous formulation) in Korea (Oct 14), a preliminary earnings disclosure (Oct 12), and a share buyback resolution (Sep 29). The earnings disclosure is the key event to verify — if operating leverage is confirmed at the scale the screener flags, the re-rating thesis becomes more defensible. **Next check:** confirm the preliminary earnings figures against DART XBRL data and monitor whether the institutional/foreign bid persists post-results.

**2. Seegene (096530.KQ) — Score 52.0, 5 evidence families**

Seegene is a molecular diagnostics company specializing in multiplex PCR kits. After pandemic-era distortions, the company is showing genuine earnings recovery: OP YoY +309.7% on a low base, with operating margin expanding +11.3pp. The quality screener (Quality Compounder) passes it on ROE of 4.8% — modest but positive after a trough year. The cycle re-rating flag here reflects margin normalization rather than a cyclical commodity call. Smart money quality and earnings screeners both fire: ₩9.5bn in net institutional/foreign buying over five days with retail selling on the other side. The DART filing is a Q3 interim dividend record date (Sep 28) — routine, but signals management confidence. **Caution:** short ratio is 7.7%, program buying is +11.1%, and the stock sits at institutional rank #310 on the Kiwoom surface — the short position is worth monitoring. **Next check:** verify whether the earnings recovery trajectory extends into H2 2026 and whether the dividend signals a structural payout policy shift.

**3. HMM (011200.KS) — Score 46.8, 6 evidence families**

HMM is Korea's largest container shipping operator. It scores six evidence families despite missing the Quality Compounder gate (not in that screener), compensating with smart money flow, consensus revision, RS80 leadership, and DART filings. The five-day foreign-plus-institutional net inflow of ₩117.4bn — against ₩180.8bn in retail selling — is one of the largest absorption figures in today's screener. Consensus estimates have been revised upward (composite z-score +0.33). Three DART filings from October 15 cover a shareholder meeting convening, a record date, and a new capex decision — the capex filing is the item to read carefully for fleet expansion or drydock implications. The RS80 not-late signal shows a pivot gap of +3.7% with a 20-day flow score of +21.97%, suggesting institutional accumulation is still in early stages rather than exhausted. The primary risk is freight rate volatility — any deterioration in trans-Pacific or Asia-Europe rates would pressure the re-rating thesis quickly. **Next check:** read the DART capex filing and cross-reference with spot container rate indices before treating the consensus revision as durable.
